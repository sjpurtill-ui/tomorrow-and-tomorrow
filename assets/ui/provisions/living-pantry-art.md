# Living Pantry artwork

`living-pantry.jpg` is the village painting selected by the user in the
Living Pantry Food-screen concept on October 16, 2026. It is reused unchanged
from the task's `home-overview-atlas.jpg` design asset.

SHA-256: `21146AE4B37F2589C2FB811F0B074D50E18333E9DC1AEB03D0028E131CA5DBF8`.

Fields, woodland and river provide a visual setting for the corresponding live
food readings. The painting is representative scenery, not an exact settlement
map, a population count, or proof that the pictured technologies are unlocked.
The Food screen's numbers, warnings and controls use the existing simulation
and command paths.
