# Lives and consequences artwork

`lives-hearth.png` is the human-centered painting approved in the Home design
preview on October 15, 2026. It was generated with the built-in imagegen tool
for this task. The original PNG is retained for large-screen image quality.

Original generation: `exec-5f8d77f5-d8c4-4d0d-bc5d-5285ac0b54d0.png` in task
`01a13b4e-53d9-7361-8d69-93b20c9c91ec`.

The painting represents early families preparing materials, teaching and caring
around a hearth beside water. Its figures are illustrative scenery, never a
census, a count of assigned workers, or evidence of a researched practice.
The Home screen takes all numerical readings from the live simulation.

The existing population-stage portraits remain available for the spare initial
camp and larger settlements; they do not unlock buildings or alter game rules.
