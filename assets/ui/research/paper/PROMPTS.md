## Exact photovoltaic generation prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Create ONE square finished technology-tree illustration for Tomorrow and Tomorrow, 1024 by 1024. Input image is a binding STYLE reference only: reproduce its spare matte gouache, dry-brush printmaking, warm ivory fibrous paper, distressed pigment grain, irregular brushed edges, flat simplified color masses, almost featureless faces and generous untouched paper. New subject: Photovoltaic Power. A modern field technician in simple muted ochre work clothes kneels beside a low tilted bank of dark slate-blue solar panels, carefully connecting its cable to a small plain electrical cabinet; a second worker stands farther back inspecting another panel. Readable restrained silhouettes, accurate recognizable photovoltaic panel grids and simple support frames. A quiet sunlit dry-grass field, low horizon fading into warm paper; most of the upper half is empty ivory, composition grounded in the lower half. Subtle dark olive vegetation and terracotta accents. This is a modern civilian energy practice, not ancient people with modern props. Same illustrative abstraction and texture as the reference; no glossy rendering, realism, glowing effects, dramatic sky, outlines, text, logos, labels, UI, borders, montage or watermark.

## Exact public-schools generation prompt

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-11, using the same approved apprentice style reference. Saved as `public_schools.png`; inspected for ancient materials, four learners, teacher, sparse composition and texture.

Use case: historical-scene. Create one square 1024x1024 technology-tree illustration for Tomorrow and Tomorrow. Input image is a STYLE reference only. Match its exact sparse matte gouache and dry-brush print illustration on warm ivory fibrous paper: simple flat silhouettes, almost featureless faces, distressed pigment grain, irregular edges, restrained dark olive, ochre, charcoal and muted terracotta. Subject: Public Schools. An early urban courtyard school: one teacher in a plain linen robe sits beside a low mudbrick wall, demonstrating a small clay tablet to four learners seated on reed mats with their own clay tablets and reed styluses. One learner carefully presses a stylus into clay. A simple sunshade pole and the corner of an unadorned mudbrick doorway suggest the shared learning place. Historically coherent ancient teaching materials, no blackboards, books or modern desks. Keep the people and teaching action in the lower third, an asymmetric grouping with abundant untouched ivory paper above. A few soft ground strokes; architecture dissolves into paper. Quiet thoughtful human gestures, not a portrait. No readable text, symbols, lettering, logos, border, frame, UI, montage, photorealism, dramatic lighting or glossy detail.
