Retrieved October 5, 2026. Both clips were converted to mono PCM16 at 22050 Hz,
DC removed, peak normalized to 0.85, and given short edge fades. Silence around
the female performance was trimmed. No time stretching, looping, or synthesized
vowels were added. Runtime makes a seeded pitch/rate adjustment of at most 2%,
normalizes to 0.6 peak and applies scene gain. Import compression must remain off
because the runtime reads PCM samples. Full source packs and tool dependencies
are excluded from the repository.
