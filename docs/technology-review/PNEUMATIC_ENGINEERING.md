# Pneumatic engineering

Six individually authored discoveries add pressure pipe jointing, cylinder boring, packed piston seals, directional air valves, pneumatic cylinders and pneumatic pressing. Their prerequisites reconverge through existing vessel fabrication, workshop standards, precision machinery, bearing surfaces, weaving, lubrication, compressed-air production and structural mechanics. No calendar unlocks. Each unlocks a physical component recipe, and pneumatic pressing also unlocks a commissioned workshop.

The six recipes consume metal, cloth, graphite and their own manufactured intermediates. Two fittings and two packings feed one complete installation chain: one fitting and packing enter the actuator, another packing enters its valve, and the final fitting connects the installed press. Tools are paid through ordinary line setup; shared Crafting work makes batches. No discovery grants equipment or labor.

A press installation costs one Pneumatic Press and one Pressure Pipe Fitting, then 12 commissioning worker-days. It uses one operator and half a batch of Compressed Air per full operating day to supply three mechanical-work service units. Fractional air or staffing scales output. Electricity is consumed upstream when compressed air is made, rather than charged twice at the press. Air already in stock can run the press during a generation interruption. Travel or absent operators stops consumption and service. Mechanical-work benefits use the existing bounded workshop production modifier; there is no new labor authority.

Directional valves control whether and where air flows; cylinders convert air pressure into piston motion. These distinctions follow the component functions described by [Festo's valve documentation](https://www.festo.com/us/en/c/products/valves-and-valve-manifolds/directional-control-valves-id_pim115) and [cylinder documentation](https://www.festo.com/us/en/c/products/actuators-and-drives/pneumatic-cylinders-id_pim135). This is an aggregate game model with abstract quantities, not a claim of engineering dimensions, historical priority or calibrated energy efficiency.

Existing owned installation and stock saves carry the new state. Operating-input validation accepts Compressed Air; maximum commissioning staffing and mechanical service bounds now cover ten plant types. Existing records are unchanged, while new identities require this catalog revision.

Limits: no pipe network, leak simulation, pressure/temperature state, moisture treatment, seal wear, tool-specific pressing outcomes or AI consumer-plant investment yet. Compressed Air is presently a stored industrial batch without idle leakage. The six discoveries are part of the incomplete 5,000-discovery catalog, not full pneumatic engineering coverage.
