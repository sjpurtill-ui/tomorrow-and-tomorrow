## Scope and semantic review

Existing dimensional analysis, numerical integration, numerical root finding and least-squares estimation were excluded from new counting. Proposed alternative root solvers and quadrature names were omitted to avoid splitting the existing generic identities without a separately reviewed operating scope. General matrix algebra, numerical conditioning, sparse methods and model validation remain existing parent concepts.

The new matrix methods have different required structure and outputs: pivoting controls elimination choices; orthogonal factors support solves and projections; singular factors expose rank/gain directions; Cholesky applies only to suitable positive-definite symmetric systems. Krylov iteration spends matrix-action work and may fail to converge; preconditioning spends setup/application work on a separate transformed problem. Arithmetic representation errors are distinguished from noisy input evidence. [GNU linear algebra documentation](https://www.gnu.org/software/gsl/doc/html/linalg.html), [Netlib iterative-method templates](https://www.netlib.org/linalg/html_templates/node1.html), and [GNU floating-point documentation](https://www.gnu.org/software/gsl/doc/html/ieee754.html) support these distinctions.

Global polynomial, local smooth spline and monotonicity-preserving interpolation are separate qualified constructions. They do not estimate hidden observations or establish safe extrapolation. [GNU interpolation documentation](https://www.gnu.org/software/gsl/doc/html/interp.html) describes their different continuity and shape properties.

Linear allocation, convex optimality checking, interior-point iteration and discrete branch-and-bound have different admissible problems, work and certificates. A feasible incumbent is not a proved discrete optimum; a convex certificate needs its assumptions. [Boyd and Vandenberghe](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/) and [SCIP branching documentation](https://www.scipopt.org/doc/html/BRANCH.php) provide primary mathematical/implementation references. Existing network flow and dynamic programming identities are retained; neither is used as a synonym for these mechanisms.

Importance sampling changes the sampling distribution with explicit weights; control variates adjust estimates using justified known expectations. Neither creates measurements or guarantees lower error. [GNU Monte Carlo documentation](https://www.gnu.org/software/gsl/doc/html/montecarlo.html) and [Glynn and Szechtman's control-variate paper](https://web.stanford.edu/~glynn/papers/2002/GSzechtman02.html) support these distinct constructions.

Bootstrap uncertainty uses resampling with replacement; permutation tests use allowed rearrangements under a null. Sequential likelihood boundaries govern accumulating evidence and stopping; survival estimation retains censoring separately from events. These are not duplicate applications of generic statistical inference, nor do they grant clinical effects. See NIST's [bootstrap](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/bootplot.htm), [permutation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/ksamperm.htm), [sequential sampling](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc254.htm), and [reliability estimation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/apr_d.htm) material.

Stochastic differential models specify drift/noise rather than deterministic certainty; bifurcation analysis locates qualitative parameter changes; Lyapunov certificates establish only a qualified stability region. Nondimensional similarity matches relevant model/target groups and requires actual comparison trials. Sources: [MIT stochastic equations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-642-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2024/resources/mit18_642_f24_lec24_pdf/), [MIT nonlinear dynamics](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.385/Notes/index.html), [MIT Lyapunov analysis](https://underactuated.mit.edu/lyapunov.html), and [MIT dimensional similarity](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-25-advanced-fluid-mechanics-fall-2013/pages/dimensional-analysis/). Sources support mechanisms; the selected game prerequisites and future operating contracts are explicit design inferences.
