## Candidate identity and paid inspection

Manufacture of fired wheels, sized abrasive web and ground/finished component candidates creates a bounded lot record in the existing per-civilization technology-operations ledger. Each record fixes original production job, ordinal range, recipe, quantity and source store. Inspection reserves one actual candidate and its consumables once, retains partial work on its ordinary production job, and pays electricity with progress. Acceptance depends on deterministic modeled size/form/surface ratios attached to the original manufactured identity. Changing inspection jobs cannot reroll a consumed candidate. Cancelling unfinished inspection sacrifices the reserved part rather than refunding it. Stocks lacking a lot record cannot gain provenance for free.

Inspections release accepted or explicitly rejected stock. Rejected wheels/belts are not serviceable tools. Selected rejected steel forms may be remelted through a paid arc-furnace recipe: 2.5 rejected forms return one Steel, below the metal input of even the lightest supported housing. The production panel reports the last decision, three normalized ratios and cumulative rejects. This model is restricted to the declared near-net shapes and selected wheel/belt configurations; it does not infer real-world fatigue life, thermal histories, microscopic defects or arbitrary materials.

The lot register holds at most256 unfinished records. Full capacity blocks production before setup payment or material consumption. Exhausted records may retire; pending paid inspection keeps its immutable identity and report. These fields live under the existing save owner, with strict structural/model checks and legacy absence allowed. This branch's intermediate candidate stock had no prior canonical save format; untraceable aggregate stock is deliberately not upgraded to accepted parts.
