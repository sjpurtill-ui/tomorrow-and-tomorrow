## Scope and primary evidence

Incident roles, cross-jurisdiction command, next-period objectives and capability typing describe who may commit which qualified resources. They do not replace the existing emergency coordination center or mutual-aid activation method. Mobilization tracking and demobilization add exclusive commitments and paid return/restoration. Mechanism context: [FEMA incident management doctrine](https://training.fema.gov/EMIWeb/IS/IS700b/Handouts/National_Incident_Management%20System_Third%20Edition_October_2017.pdf), [FEMA resource management](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/a-z/nims/managing-resources.html).

Evacuation zoning, phased release, assisted transport, shelter-in-place assessment, destination balancing and staged re-entry are separate decisions. Their operating model must retain route congestion, current protection evidence, finite accessible transport and supplied destination capacity. Care during displacement already exists as its own authored identity and is not counted again. Mechanism context: [FEMA evacuation and shelter-in-place planning](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/fema_DRRA-1209-planning-considerations-evacuation-shelter-in-place_guide.pdf).

Damage assessment acquires dated evidence; severity classification makes observations comparable. Separating asset damage, interrupted flows and recovery needs prevents double-counted accounts. Reports retain unknown locations and disagreement rather than revealing the entire map. Mechanism context: [FEMA damage assessment course](https://emilms.fema.gov/is_0559/groups/211.html), [UNDRR recovery planning](https://www.undrr.org/publication/words-action-guidelines-build-back-better-recovery-rehabilitation-and-reconstruction).

Debris clearance priorities, stream segregation, temporary-site management and load verification consume different operational evidence. Opening a link does not dispose of its debris; a full temporary site cannot accept another load. Verified receipts and measured loads support existing procurement acceptance rather than authorizing automatic payment. Mechanism context: [FEMA debris planning](https://emilms.fema.gov/is_0558/groups/42.html), [FEMA debris monitoring capability](https://rtlt.preptoolkit.fema.gov/Public/Resource/View/7-508-1234?q=public+works).

Recovery project prioritization weighs observed needs against available funding and work; pre-disaster agreements establish participating authorities and contingent commitments before disruption. Neither duplicates actual appropriation or creates funds. Mechanism context: [UNDRR recovery planning](https://www.undrr.org/publication/words-action-guidelines-build-back-better-recovery-rehabilitation-and-reconstruction), [UNDRR recovery readiness framework](https://www.undrr.org/publication/documents-and-publications/recovery-readiness-framework-and-assessment-tool).
