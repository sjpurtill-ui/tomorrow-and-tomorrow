# Production evidence in historical pacing reports

Schema 10 adds a compact `production` object to initial, annual and final snapshots. It records the number of currently available civilian recipes, retained civilian lines, manufactured-stock kinds, installed units, units under construction, whether the operating ledger belongs to the current day and remaining daily services. The final bottleneck report includes recipe identities with new-line blockers, retained line states and partial worker-day progress, installation records and manufactured-stock quantities.

These observations distinguish known production methods from commissioned machinery. They do not infer that a stock was locally manufactured, count discarded/retooled lines as lifetime output, or interpret a nominal Working line state as guaranteed throughput. Startup blockers cover the ordinary setup/material/workforce checks; they do not include a free production slot or future electricity supply. Reported services are what remains after consumption, not total daily generation. A stale daily ledger cannot be reported as current running service. Stored energy remains a quantity of stored energy, not a generation claim.

The helper reads the scoped actor's existing production and operation rules. It grants no knowledge, stock, energy or workers. The active schema-9 long diagnostic retains the code it loaded; new fields apply only to newly launched runs. No restart or target completion is implied. This remains an isolated-seat diagnostic with synthetic local water and no foreign exchange, not full-world or millennial proof.
