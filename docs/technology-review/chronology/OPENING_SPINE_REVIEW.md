## Chronology exceptions requiring repair

### 1. Fire is absent as a common foundation

The live tree can begin hearth cooking, charcoal production, smoke preservation,
and pit firing without first establishing how fire is maintained or deliberately
produced. This is a causal omission rather than a pacing problem.

**Repair:** promote the already-authored Ember Tending and Friction Fire Ignition
identities. Keep Percussion Fire Ignition as an alternate ignition method. Make
controlled heat a common foundation for cooking, charcoal, smoking, and firing,
while preserving distinct material and process requirements.

### 2. Too many unrelated roots can open together

The live opening contains plausible root practices across many research channels.
Because they are all structurally ready, a civilization can begin several unrelated
investigations immediately and receive a cluster of supposedly rare breakthroughs.

**Repair:** expose a root question through actual repeated activity or evidence:
rain on occupied ground for drainage, guarded nights for watch rotation, recurring
store disputes for tallies, plant cases for medicinal classification, and so on.
This is an opportunity requirement, not a calendar gate.

### 3. Cooking and firing skip controlled heat

Hearth Roasting Control is a root. Smoke Preservation can follow Food Drying alone.
An alternate Pit Firing route treats Clay Shaping plus Food Drying as sufficient.

**Repair:** require the fire foundation while leaving preparation, smoking, and
ceramic firing as distinct investigations with different inputs and consequences.

### 4. Opening sewing depends on a post-siege material source

Bone-Needle Sewing currently obtains bone only from post-siege hunting recovery and
its operating contract assumes cloth or yarn. That cannot carry ordinary early
hide, sinew, or coarse-fiber sewing.

**Repair:** provide a normal bounded hunting or food-processing bone supply and an
early compatible material route. Later cloth and yarn sewing remain improvements.

### 5. Selective planting compresses the cultivation bootstrap

Seasonal Patterns plus recognized fertile soil currently lead directly to Selective
Planting. The identity implicitly assumes sowing, harvest, retained seed, and
comparison across plantings.

**Repair:** make ordinary cultivation practice and retained seed evidence explicit
operating foundations. Do not create separate technologies for every farming action
unless they represent distinct teachable mechanisms.

### 6. Animal Taming is too broad for its foundation

Seasonal Patterns alone currently supports raising herd-animal young that stay,
breed, and follow. Suitable animals, capture, feeding, protection, handling, and
breeding continuity are implicit.

**Repair:** retain Animal Taming as the anchor but require an encountered suitable
population and a sustained husbandry program. Pack animals and mounts remain later
specializations rather than automatic consequences.

### 7. Knowledge sometimes receives transformation credit before operation

Public Stores and Framed Construction have credible knowledge foundations, but the
historical transformation requires a maintained reserve or completed durable
structure. Knowledge alone should not move the civilization's hidden state.

**Repair:** evaluate hidden transformation state from operating records, adoption,
staff, physical assets, and continuity. Discovery remains necessary but is not
sufficient.

## Proposed first implementation wave

**Wave O1: fire, food, tools, and the first durable camp**

The wave contains 22 connected identities: the six T01 material and environmental
anchors most directly used by camp work; the five authored fire identities; Hearth
Roasting Control, Controlled Flaking, Hafted Tools, Twisted Cordage, Bone-Needle
Sewing, Wood Joinery, Food Drying, Smoke Preservation, Basketry, Clay Vessels, and
Pit Firing.

The player-visible result should be:

- fire that must first be found or preserved, and later can be deliberately made;
- fuel, tending work, heat control, smoke, and fire danger as real constraints;
- cooking, drying, smoking, and firing that use the same fire capability but remain
  distinct processes;
- tools, bindings, containers, coverings, and camp structures made from encountered
  materials and maintained through ordinary work; and
- a sparse hierarchy of landmark announcements, compact notices, and program
  reports rather than one modal panel for every method.

This wave should not yet change the global discovery-rate curve. It first makes the
opening causal world and activity loop coherent. Research pacing is adjusted only
after the resulting opening can be played without relying on frequent discoveries.
