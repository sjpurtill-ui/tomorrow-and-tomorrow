# Early-game experience while discovery is slow

The opening centuries cannot be a research waiting room. Formal discoveries are
rare, while decisions, journeys, relationships, environmental evidence, projects,
and consequences remain frequent.

## Player promise

The player begins with a vulnerable human community, an incompletely known world,
and limited ability to preserve knowledge. The opening is about making a people
and a place durable. Technology changes that struggle, but the struggle already
contains meaningful play.

## The active loop

1. **Observe:** weather, water, foods, animals, illness, materials, travelers,
   neighboring groups, and internal tensions produce incomplete evidence.
2. **Choose an objective:** explore, move, store, build, cultivate, negotiate,
   train, heal, defend, celebrate, or investigate.
3. **Commit people and supplies:** capable people leave other work; provisions,
   tools, gifts, and political support are finite.
4. **Follow the attempt:** journeys take time, projects expose dependencies, and
   leaders report choices or ask for direction at meaningful forks.
5. **Live with the result:** the community gains a place, relationship, practice,
   obligation, injury, rivalry, story, partial clue, or rare breakthrough.

This loop should produce decisions at a comfortable play cadence even when no
new discovery completes.

## Sources of opening drama

### Geography and movement

- Select seasonal camps, crossings, water access, defensive ground, and eventual
  settlement sites from incomplete knowledge.
- Send scouts with explicit purposes and finite provisions.
- Decide whether to revisit, migrate, divide the community, or accept a hazardous
  route for a valuable material or contact.
- Accumulate a human map through reports rather than beginning with a technical
  survey interface.

### Seasons and reserves

- Anticipate lean periods, storms, heat, floods, animal movement, and harvests.
- Choose immediate consumption against seed, breeding, travel, gift, and reserve
  needs.
- Recover from spoilage or shortage through altered behavior and relationships,
  rather than only through a global production penalty.

### People and authority

- Recognize practitioners, organizers, healers, scouts, negotiators, storytellers,
  and fighters through demonstrated work.
- Handle disputes about movement, stores, risk, labor, obligations, and leadership.
- Make customs and offices emerge from repeated needs before formal institutions
  exist.
- Let deaths, injuries, loyalties, and rivalries matter without making one person
  the owner of aggregate population labor.

### Contact and exchange

- Encounter strangers through travel and reports.
- Exchange food, materials, access, marriage ties, hostages, protection, stories,
  demonstrations, and obligations.
- Decide whether foreign expertise should be invited, imitated, purchased, hidden,
  or resisted.
- Make peaceful dependence and hostile appropriation materially different.

### Projects

- Build shelters, stores, paths, crossings, water works, gathering places, defenses,
  workshops, and boats as visible commitments of labor and material.
- Allow partial projects, changing conditions, repair, and abandonment.
- Let projects create observations that can support later inquiry without silently
  granting the discovery.

### Crises

- Use drought, severe seasons, fire, illness, predation, accidents, displacement,
  raids, and internal fracture sparingly and causally.
- Give preparation and relationships time to matter before resolution.
- Avoid turning the opening into continuous random punishment.

## Rare breakthroughs

An H01 breakthrough should change several decisions. It should not exist primarily
as a percentage modifier.

Examples:

| Breakthrough family | Immediate change | New vulnerability or obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal understanding | Better expedition and reserve timing | Incorrect expectations remain dangerous in unusual years. |
| Food preservation | Longer reserves, journeys, and exchange | Storage labor, fuel, containers, spoilage, and theft matter. |
| Containers | Bulk collection, transport, and separation | Production, repair, cleaning, and material supply are required. |
| Water-site knowledge | Safer camps and settlement candidates | Wells and water places require protection and maintenance. |
| Cultivation | Greater future food potential | Seed continuity, land, labor, weather, and tenure create new risks. |
| Durable construction | Longer-lived places and stores | Material extraction, repair, sanitation, and political claims grow. |
| Organized watch or drill | Better warning and coordination | Training and provisioning consume time; command creates political power. |

## Presentation hierarchy

| Tier | Player treatment | Intended frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landmark breakthrough | Full art-led announcement and explicit account of changed capabilities | Rare; only transformation-defining work |
| Significant discovery | Compact notice, relevant decisions, archive entry | Occasional |
| Supporting method | Added to the active program or workshop report | Regular once institutions grow |
| Refinement or standard | Periodic digest and searchable archive | Frequent in later play; never a modal interruption by default |

The exact frequency is tuned after the opening loop is playable. The hierarchy is
decided before tuning so that catalog size does not dictate interruption count.

## First playable slice

The first chronology implementation slice covers T01–T04 and ends when a community
can sustain a defensible permanent settlement with managed subsistence, stores,
water, basic construction, remembered routes, and relationships beyond itself.

The slice is ready for review when:

- the player has meaningful objectives between breakthroughs;
- at least two viable settlement and subsistence strategies exist;
- migration and staying both have consequences;
- foreign contact can help without instantly transferring mastery;
- rare breakthroughs alter actual operations and choices;
- loss and delay remain recoverable; and
- no discovery panel cadence is required to keep the opening interesting.
