# Road vehicle and infrastructure coverage

32 authored D09 proposals describe distinct drivetrain mechanisms, control functions, tire construction/measurement, impact evidence, emissions treatment and road/bridge assessment. None is working runtime content. Existing broad motor vehicles, transmissions, suspension, hydraulic brakes, pavement construction and maintenance are not recounted as new identities.

Differential gearing changes wheel-speed relationships; constant-velocity joints handle articulation; synchronizers, hydraulic actuation, variable pulleys, hydrodynamic coupling and dual-clutch control each require different equipment and trials. Regenerative brake allocation depends on actual battery acceptance. Anti-lock, traction and yaw control share infrastructure but measure and act on different failures; no controller creates unavailable tire grip.

Crash load paths, restraint force limitation and test instrumentation remain separate from one another. Tire warnings and wheel balancing do not repair tire structures. Exhaust catalyst, particulate filtering, reagent-mediated nitrogen-oxide treatment and vapor adsorption retain distinct inputs and pollutant limits. Diagnostic evidence requires finite observations and does not automatically repair faults. Road friction, fatigue, rutting, axle measurement, bearing movement and scour records retain their specific measurement domains and downstream uses.

All rows require paid equipment/materials or actual observations, work and qualification before a named downstream consumer can use them. Quantitative performance must remain bound to tests and current operating conditions, not universal safety bonuses. Imported qualified components can serve a known assembly process without granting local manufacture. Five paid external-learning contracts accompany each row. Sources support short mechanisms; prerequisites and game-state contracts are design inferences. Horizon classifications describe the selected operational capability, not exclusive invention dates. No numerical vehicle engineering instructions or claims of regulatory certification are supplied.

References are manufacturer technical explanations, original research/manuals and official infrastructure documents. The EPA-hosted three-way catalyst record identifies an original technical chapter; proposed game behavior is deliberately narrower than broad emissions elimination. The NHTSA event-data page distinguishes test instrumentation from production event recorders; the authored instrumentation row concerns controlled trials.
