# AI research studies and visiting scholars

AI monthly foreign-policy reviews can now send a paid research delegation. A candidate must come from a collection physically returned and fully examined locally, identify its source society, and concern a still-unresolved question with valid local foundations. Formal study purchasing requires Experimental Controls and Public Schools. Before that combination is available, apprenticeship contracts or public schools permit a paid scholar invitation. Both modes require local Knowledge staff. The planner never reads the supplier's hidden discoveries, adoption or workforce. A historical report justifies asking; it does not guarantee acceptance.

For formal studies, the ordinary purchase transaction quotes a confirmed route, reserves payment and travel provisions, negotiates with the actual supplier on arrival, brings accepted studies home and requires local examination. Refused payment follows the existing once-only refund path. No discovery, collection, staff or resources are granted by the planner. The `research_purchase` and `research_scholar` civilization commands use the normal order dispatcher; AI autonomy remains inside the existing AI-controller guard.

The planner prioritizes active investigations, shorter trips and a smaller fraction of stock committed. It selects only non-food gifts costing at most ten percent of the corresponding material stock. Hunger, war, an existing embassy or absent research staff suppress purchasing. It waits 365 days after a research mission for the same source/subject, using existing diplomatic history; this is a retry policy, not a technology calendar gate. An already studied collection of purchase-level strength for that subject prevents redundant procurement even if weaker collections also exist. Knowledge gained locally makes the question ineligible.

A scholar invitation uses the existing visiting-teacher transaction. It reserves the teacher’s travel and 60-day board in addition to the envoys’ provisions and material payment. Source acceptance, actual absence from the source workforce, travel, local staffing, teaching duration and eventual return remain governed by ScholarVisits. A pending visit for the subject prevents another invitation. The planner never supplies a teacher directly.

Limitations: this follows examined returned knowledge; it does not invent leads, discover suppliers omnisciently, negotiate licenses, launch partnerships or purchase production materials. Once formal purchasing is available, an active inquiry can still use a scholar if the purchase quote is unavailable or the optimistic local examination delay after return is at least the 60-day teaching window. Otherwise it prefers the durable purchased study. The estimate allows the existing returned backlog to clear during travel, adds the new study’s 120 work, and uses current Knowledge staff, food intake and maximum printed support. Assuming printed support even without stock makes the long-delay decision conservative. A failed scholar quote falls back to a valid purchase. Inactive questions retain the durable study preference. This is a workload policy, not an exact forecast: it does not predict inquiry completion, staffing changes, incoming collections or supplier acceptance. The reserve fraction, annual retry delay and ranking weights are gameplay choices. Diplomatic history has its existing bound, so an old retry record can eventually age out. No separate acquisition save ledger was introduced. Competition with other peaceful missions is a policy choice: an eligible research mission is considered before ordinary peaceful relationship actions; war and hunger suppress it. Native player integration and long-campaign behavior remain unverified.
