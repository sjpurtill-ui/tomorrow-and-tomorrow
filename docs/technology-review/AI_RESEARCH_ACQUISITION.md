# AI commissioning of stronger research

AI monthly foreign-policy reviews can now send a paid research delegation. A candidate must come from a collection physically returned and fully examined locally, identify its source society, and concern a still-unresolved question with valid local foundations. Experimental Controls, Public Schools and available Knowledge workers remain required. The planner never reads the supplier's hidden discoveries, adoption or workforce. A historical report justifies asking; it does not guarantee acceptance.

The ordinary purchase transaction quotes a confirmed route, reserves payment and travel provisions, negotiates with the actual supplier on arrival, brings accepted studies home and requires local examination. Refused payment follows the existing once-only refund path. No discovery, collection, staff or resources are granted by the planner. The same new `research_purchase` civilization command is available to the normal order dispatcher; AI autonomy remains inside the existing AI-controller guard.

The planner prioritizes active investigations, shorter trips and a smaller fraction of stock committed. It selects only non-food gifts costing at most ten percent of the corresponding material stock. Hunger, war, an existing embassy or absent research staff suppress purchasing. It waits 365 days after a research mission for the same source/subject, using existing diplomatic history; this is a retry policy, not a technology calendar gate. An already studied collection of purchase-level strength for that subject prevents redundant procurement even if weaker collections also exist. Knowledge gained locally makes the question ineligible.

Limitations: this commissions stronger versions of returned knowledge; it does not invent leads, discover suppliers omnisciently, request scholars, negotiate licenses, launch partnerships or purchase production materials. The reserve fraction, annual retry delay and ranking weights are gameplay choices. Diplomatic history has its existing bound, so an old retry record can eventually age out. No separate acquisition save ledger was introduced. Competition with other peaceful missions is a policy choice: an eligible research mission is considered before ordinary peaceful relationship actions; war and hunger suppress it. Native player integration and long-campaign behavior remain unverified.
