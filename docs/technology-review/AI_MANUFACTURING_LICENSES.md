# AI manufacturing licenses

Monthly foreign decisions can negotiate paid civilian manufacturing licenses. Existing supplied, unpaused, below-target dependent lines receive priority. The planner also considers a first Paper or Printed Sheets process when unfinished returned studies need supplies, no currently available production plan can satisfy that need, and one batch plus real tooling is already in stores. Local mastery makes a license unnecessary. Existing paper and printed stock are credited against remaining study work.

Operating cultivation can also motivate new fertilizer licenses without requiring currently assigned Knowledge workers. The manufacturing and acquisition planners share the same observed harvest, local nutrient reserves and fertilizer-stock accounting. For each deficient nutrient, an available domestic supply route is preferred. A missing route can be replaced by a fully examined license prospect only when one batch and setup tools are already supplied. Both nitrogen and phosphorus must have a useful stock, domestic route or examined, supplied license prospect before the planner commits to this complementary chain. Two missing methods can therefore be negotiated on successive real embassy trips; the first contract does not instantly grant the second. The scarcer nutrient gets a modest demand priority, below the priority for an existing dependent production line.

Supplier leads come from physically returned, fully examined collections or the civilization's own earlier production contracts. New candidates do not inspect the supplier's hidden discoveries, adoption or workforce; the ordinary licensing negotiation can still be refused on arrival. Existing contract support is checked by the existing contract system. The normal `research_license` order reserves material payment and embassy provisions, travels, negotiates and delivers the contract. No knowledge, adoption, license, worker or manufactured output is created by the planner.

The existing license terms remain: 365 days from return, 65% independent throughput, actual materials/tooling/power and staff, interruption by supplier withdrawal or war, and renewal only in the final 90 days while active. Material gifts are non-food and at most ten percent of the corresponding stock. Hunger, war, an existing embassy and absent Crafting staff prevent a new request. A 30-day source/subject retry window uses existing licensing mission history. This differs from the annual research-study retry policy so a contract can be renewed before expiry. Prior contracts provide renewal leads even if no collection was retained.

Limitations: new-license demand currently covers immediately supplied paper, printing and fertilizer, while existing dependent lines can renew any supported civilian recipe. The planner does not procure missing raw inputs, license military doctrine or plant installation, forecast future prices, reserve supplier staff, negotiate a better rate, or create a first powered licensed process without available electricity. An affordable existing production plan is preferred over a new license; this is a conservative dependency policy, not an optimized profit calculation. Partnership automation and imported services remain unfinished. The controller checks licenses before scholar/study acquisition and ordinary peaceful missions, so those actions compete for the same physical embassy.

A test negotiates both mineral fertilizer processes through separate paid trips, manufactures them at 65% throughput, and applies their output to cultivation while both production discoveries remain unknown locally. Tests also cover unexamined/not-yet-returned prospects, no hidden supplier-knowledge query, domestic alternatives, missing complementary inputs, idle farming and travel. Potential negotiations can still be refused; the planner does not guarantee two suppliers will accept, that the second agreement will arrive before the first expires, or that all future replenishment will remain available.

No separate acquisition ledger or save schema was added. Payments, contract expiry and diplomatic history use existing saved records. No canonical integration or long-campaign acceptance is claimed.


Operating machinery can also justify a new license prospect. The planner shares the civilian operating-input needs calculation, respects paused input lines, and requires examined returned evidence, supplied tooling and an existing usable source of power. Local manufacture takes precedence. A paid compressor license can feed an installed pneumatic press through reduced-rate air production without granting the compressor discovery. It does not negotiate raw-material imports or build generation in anticipation of an unknown licensed method.
