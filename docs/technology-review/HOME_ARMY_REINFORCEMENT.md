# Reinforcing armies stationed at home

A new ordinary order transfers a requested unit type from the trained home pool into an existing field army stationed at `player_home`. The monthly controller uses it for a researched doctrine's missing supporting arm. Requests are bounded by the actual trained unit count at home; they do not raise recruits, manufacture equipment or create a new army. One eligible supporting contingent is considered per military decision.

The operation reuses the existing proportional detachment path with an optional unit filter. Personnel, issued equipment and ammunition move together. The remaining home formation keeps the complement. Practice, training and experience travel with the detached formation. Destination formation IDs are assigned without colliding inside the army. Derived army statistics are rebuilt, while its identity, commander, position, objective metadata and casualty pools remain in place. Wounded personnel stay in their existing pools. No provisions or marching speed are granted.

The transfer rejects invalid requests, unavailable/occupied or traveling home settlements, and field armies that are moving, distant, embarked or committed to battle. This uses the engine's stationed-home location state, consistent with its existing home reorganization rules. It is not a route simulation or distant reinforcement convoy. Both source and destination must be available for home reorganization. Supporting formations still need ordinary supplies, equipment and rehearsal before doctrine provides its full benefit. No tactical controls or mandatory player logistics flow are introduced.

Old callers of the detachment helper keep their existing behavior because the new filter defaults to empty. Existing army/formation records carry the result with no new save fields. The automatic planner handles supporting arms, not general force replacement, upgrading old field-army composition, remote attachments or competing theater priorities. Those remain unfinished parts of full-history military development.
