# Textile manufacture and care supplies

Six authored discoveries add a physical plant-fiber chain: Fiber Retting, Fiber Combing, Drop Spindles, Warp-Weighted Looms, Plain Weaving and Woven Dressings. Seven workshop recipes produce Prepared Fibers, Combed Fibers, Spun Yarn (two routes), Loom Weights, Woven Cloth and Woven Dressings. Each recipe has actual consumed feedstock, paid tooling and finite workshop work. Prepared fibers can be spun directly; combing is an optional intermediate with a different work/material tradeoff. No calendar gate is used.

| Product | Consumed materials per batch | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Prepared Fibers | 2 Fiber Plants, 2 Freshwater | 3 |
| Combed Fibers | 1 Prepared Fibers | 1.5 |
| Spun Yarn | 1 Prepared Fibers | 3 |
| Spun Yarn, combed route | 0.75 Combed Fibers | 2.5 |
| Loom Weights | 1 Clay, 0.5 Timber | 2 |
| Woven Cloth | 2 Spun Yarn | 4 |
| Woven Dressings | 0.2 Woven Cloth, 1 Freshwater | 2 |

Tooling is additional: the cloth line consumes two Loom Weights and four Timber at setup, for example. Workshop slots and ordinary crafting allocation remain limiting. Persistent lines retain their slots when they reach their stock targets; a ruler can close a finished line through the existing command to repurpose workshop capacity. These batches and costs are game coefficients, not historical units or measured yields.

Equipped medical detachments use 0.02 Woven Dressings per supported case when available, then cover remaining cases with 0.1 Fiber Plants each. Every case still needs 0.05 Medicinal Plants, trained equipped staff, supporting provisions and recoverable wounded. Dressings do not improve the recovery coefficient or grant care on their own. Imported dressings can be used without domestic manufacturing knowledge. Quotes are read-only and actual care debits only the selected supplies. The prior raw-fiber route remains available.

This is not a sterile supply, microbiology or clinical outcome model. Retting and washing consume abstract Freshwater stocks without modeling effluent, water quality or plant species. Cloth presently supports dressings; clothing, sailmaking, dyeing and mechanized textile manufacture remain additional work. Loom weights are production tooling, not individually placed buildings. Existing stock/production save shapes carry the new IDs; older builds cannot operate the new recipes.

Historical distinctions are supported by the [Met's plant-fiber textile exhibition](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2016/plant-fibers), its [ancient sheer linen example](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/545138), and a [British Museum spindle whorl](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_1916-1016-36). They support the separation of fiber preparation, spun thread, tools and woven cloth, not the game's prerequisite order or coefficients.
