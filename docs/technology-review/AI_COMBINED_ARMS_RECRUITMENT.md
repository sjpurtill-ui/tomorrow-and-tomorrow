# Autonomous supporting-arm recruitment

The monthly controller first chooses its preferred land unit using the existing personality, research and supply checks. It now checks whether an adopted combined-arms practice calls for a missing supporting arm among forces available for home preparation. It may assign the next training contingent to that support role instead. The ordinary training and equipment-production orders remain the only mutation paths.

Desired support uses the same half-per-target ratio as the combat doctrine coverage calculation. Existing troop counts and committed trainees count toward composition, even if they are not yet ready for combat; this prevents repeatedly recruiting replacements for crews that only need training or equipment. Requests are capped at the actual unassigned recruit pool and the remaining support gap. No population or inventory is granted. Established unit/equipment training gates are required for support planning; experimental cohorts are excluded. Research adoption below 25%, hunger, suspended training, absent target formations or unavailable equipment/production prevent a support recommendation.

With no support need, existing preferred-unit behavior continues. With a support need, only the bounded contingent enters training on this decision; remaining recruits wait for a subsequent decision. Production targets follow that contingent rather than ordering an entire army's worth of support equipment. Already paused production lines remain paused through the existing line helper. Supplying one batch or stored item establishes feasibility, not a promise that every requested trainee can be equipped immediately. Training and delivery still take real time.

This plans aggregate composition around home-preparation forces and queued training. A subsequent home-reinforcement step can attach missing supporting formations to armies stationed at home; see [Home army reinforcement](HOME_ARMY_REINFORCEMENT.md). It does not move reinforcements to distant generals, forecast enemy composition, force full doctrine readiness or redesign unit rosters. Nominal composition is separate from the combat system's effective strength and rehearsal checks. Generals retain operation and battlefield authority. Government retains population and labor ownership. No save fields are added.
