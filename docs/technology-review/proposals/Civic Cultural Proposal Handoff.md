## Operating and recovery contract

Every proposal specifies a paid local operation, retained evidence, a failed/inconclusive outcome and a downstream use. Named resources are design inputs, not claims that stock IDs or facilities already exist. Runtime delivery must bind finite supplies, staff, travel, authority and consumers before counting a discovery as operating. No percentage bonus substitutes for those operations.

Governance knowledge never enacts policy, creates officials or grants consent. GovernmentPeopleSystem retains civic officials and labor. Proposed courts and custodians must use existing owners or an explicitly reviewed future extension. Cultural learning never creates skilled population or ranks languages/artistic traditions. All ALL prerequisites plus one member of every OR group remain necessary across acquisition routes.
