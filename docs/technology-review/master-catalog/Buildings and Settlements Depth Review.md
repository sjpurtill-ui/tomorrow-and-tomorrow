# Buildings and Settlements Depth Review

This section adds 57 D08 proposals after excluding three repeated inspection or disassembly applications. It deepens masonry, timber, concrete, foundations, enclosure performance and building services. At this publication checkpoint the catalog accounts for **2,499 identities: 556 implemented baseline, 48 pending communications and 1,895 master drafts**. **2,501 remain to author.** Buildings accounts for 130 of its 220 editorial target, leaving 90.

These are authored mechanisms and operating contracts, not constructed buildings or installed services. Materials, temporary works, land, skilled crews, construction time, utilities and continuing inspection remain real requirements.

## Shared arches and causal correction

The earlier load-bearing arch candidate lacked a shared construction mechanism between bridge and roof applications. Voussoir Arch Assembly, Masonry Arch Centering and Masonry Thrust Assessment now describe shared assembly, temporary support and evaluated load paths. Qualified timber or earth temporary support provides alternative construction traditions.

Masonry Bridge Arches and Vaulted Masonry Roofs now both depend directly on shared voussoir assembly. A vaulted roof no longer requires the society to build an arch bridge first. Their different applications retain their own foundation, thrust, traffic or enclosure demands. Corbelled construction is a separate mechanism and is not silently treated as identical to a true arch.

The candidate is now mapped, and the two earlier JSON/TSV parent lists have been updated. This is authored causal reconciliation, not proof of runtime construction or full historical validation. Two atlas subjects remain partial: reusable launch vehicles and reusable orbital stages.

Building Condition Surveys, Reversible Building Connections and Robotic Building Inspection were excluded as applications of existing retrofit assessment, material disassembly and robotic field inspection/repair. Those capabilities remain required without new discovery counts.

## Physical construction and outside knowledge

All records propose paid visiting instruction, reciprocal project evidence, commissioned design research, licensed/imported construction capacity and examination of accessible structures or records. Travel, transport, compensation, finite local practice and actual construction remain costs. Purchased drawings neither construct a building nor establish the condition of concealed supports.

A society may hire qualified outside masons to construct a supported arch while local apprentices learn. The project consumes actual materials, temporary support, labor and time. Continuing dependence on outside expertise affects later repair and expansion; ownership of a completed structure does not grant every construction discovery instantly.

Building services consume real resources after construction. Ventilation needs an actual flow path and appropriate equipment; heat recovery does not create heat. Emergency lighting lasts only as long as the usable reserve supply and maintained circuits permit. Suppression water must exist at the required service condition. A published plan or discovered method does not supply missing capacity.

Airtightness, intentional ventilation, rain drainage, capillary transfer and vapor diffusion describe different physical mechanisms. Solving one does not automatically solve the others. Structural strength, vibration comfort, fire behavior and post-damage load paths also remain separate assessed properties. The game should expose consequential limits without forcing the player to perform routine engineering calculations.

Construction work, resource quantities, structure condition, occupied capacity, operating service dispatch, acquisitions and AI investment remain to implement. GovernmentPeopleSystem retains daily labor ownership and aggregate population; these records create no competing settlement authority.

## Evidence grounding

The National Park Service emphasizes masonry and mortar compatibility, including the harm that unsuitable hard repair mortar can cause to softer historic masonry. This supports evaluated repair compatibility rather than a universal stronger-material upgrade. [NPS masonry preservation](https://www.nps.gov/saan/learn/upload/FinalBestPracticesInStoneBuildingPreservationManagement-2.pdf), [NPS mortar compatibility](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/mortar-unsung-hero-of-history.htm).

DOE discusses enclosure design together with daylight, heat flow, insulation continuity and moisture management. The draft distinguishes those mechanisms and their operating interactions. [DOE enclosure considerations](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/zeb-technologies-building-envelope-architectural-considerations), [DOE envelope](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/opaque-envelope).

NIST's structural-robustness work evaluates behavior following local damage rather than treating ordinary design strength as proof against disproportionate collapse. The load-path redundancy proposal therefore retains specified failure assumptions and qualified construction. [NIST structural robustness](https://www.nist.gov/el/mssd/structural-robustness-computational-modeling).

These sources support selected distinctions, not every proposed prerequisite, historical date or engineering performance claim. This is high-level game capability design rather than construction instructions. Broader technical and semantic review remains necessary.
