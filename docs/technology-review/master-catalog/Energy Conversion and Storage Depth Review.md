# Energy Conversion and Storage Depth Review

This section adds 38 proposed discoveries to the 5,000-discovery design. It changes no runtime capabilities. The resulting catalog count is 3,031 identities: 556 implemented baseline, 48 unfinished communications and 2,427 authored drafts; 1,969 remain to author. These are publication-checkpoint counts; coverage.json tracks subsequent changes.

The new scope spans maintained fire and independent ignition, industrial fuel conversion, alternative electrolyzers and storage media, marine power, reactor lifecycle work and conditional fusion fuel/heat systems. Energy carriers retain the energy cost of their production; power rating, stored energy, conversion loss and supply rate are separate quantities.

## Branching and practical limits

Friction and percussion ignition are independent causal routes; tending an ember can begin with naturally acquired or transferred fire. Cooking remains an empirical root practice with actual fire as an operating condition. Fuel air drying requires time and weather, not formal calorimetry. Hearth heat retention concerns the heating installation; insulated food cookers concern the food vessel and cooking process.

Imported ammonia or conditioned synthesis gas can support local conversion without requiring domestic upstream manufacturing. A purchased carrier does not disclose its production method. Wave machinery can supply mechanical work before an electrical consumer exists, and an oscillating water column does not require a combustion gas turbine. Solid electrolyte development can proceed through material-phase evidence or ion-transport investigation. These branches concern learning; every apparatus still needs compatible physical components.

Fusion fuel breeding uses neutron-interaction evidence without a mandatory prior fission plant. Breeding, recoverable inventory, exhaust heat management and blanket heat transfer are distinct mechanisms. Net electric delivery remains the acceptance condition of the existing Fusion Power Integration discovery. No second discovery is added for a successful demonstration. Hypothetical plant performance, closed fuel supply, lifetime and commercial output remain unproven until measured.

## Foreign learning and operating dependence

Each JSON record specifies paid scholar, partnership, purchased research, licensed/imported service and reverse-engineering terms. Outside evidence still requires finite local demonstration with the named equipment and inputs. Research purchases buy a bounded investigation, not a guaranteed discovery. A supplier can provide power or fuel without transferring generation knowledge; service ends when the paid supply or capacity ends.
