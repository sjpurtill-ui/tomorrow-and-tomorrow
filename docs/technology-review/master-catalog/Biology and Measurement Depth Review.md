# Biology and Measurement Depth Review

This section adds 59 proposals: 58 D15 biological mechanisms and one shared D06 microfluidic device mechanism. Two repeated archive/model-review applications were excluded. At this publication checkpoint the catalog accounts for **2,558 identities: 556 implemented baseline, 48 pending communications and 1,954 master drafts**. **2,442 remain to author.** Biology accounts for 119 of its 200 editorial target, leaving 81; machinery gains one shared mechanism rather than a copy for each biological application.

These are authored research and measurement capabilities, not working laboratories, treatments or altered organisms. Actual specimens, instruments, utilities, trained researchers and qualified interpretation remain necessary. Biological results do not automatically increase population health, crop yield or inherited traits.

## Evidence before application

Anatomical resemblance, functional observation, an explanatory model and a clinically or agriculturally useful intervention are different claims. The catalog now separates circulation, gas exchange, membrane transport, neural signaling, inherited variation and molecular measurements rather than compressing them into a general biology bonus.

Phylogenetic inference can use anatomical characters before sequencing exists. Organ-level gas exchange can be studied before a complete cellular energy model. Empirical plant-water observation can precede detailed photosynthesis theory. Later instruments refine supported observations; they do not make earlier evidence impossible.

Longitudinal Biobank Stewardship repeats the existing biological-archive mechanism. Biological Model Transfer Review repeats general model validation. Their constraints remain applicable without additional discovery identities. The scope file also distinguishes general separation and expression measurement from their specific physical methods.

## Outside research and finite laboratory capacity

Every record proposes paid visiting scholars, reciprocal research, a commissioned question, licensed/imported analytical service and specimen or record examination. All require competent sources, actual delivery, compensation or contribution, local interpretation and continuing inputs. A laboratory result can help answer a local question without transferring the provider's instrument-making or analytical expertise.

A society may purchase qualified sequence measurements before it can operate its own sequencing facility. It must provide suitable material and context, pay delivery and service costs, and interpret the result with its actual knowledge. Limited supplier access constrains throughput. Local instruments require their own capacity, consumables, maintenance and staff; receiving data does not create them.

Measured association is not automatically causation. A molecular marker does not guarantee a useful intervention; epigenetic measurements do not imply every change is inherited across generations. Model organisms and tissue models support bounded questions rather than replacing whole-organism or application-specific evidence. Organ-on-chip work consumes actual cells, media, devices and flow services and does not automatically supply transplant organs.

All work remains benign high-level research design. These descriptions are not experimental protocols. Runtime sample inventories, qualified facility services, analytical work budgets, application evidence, acquisition penalties and AI decisions remain unfinished.

## Evidence grounding

NHGRI distinguishes gene expression from the DNA sequence itself and describes epigenetic changes associated with DNA and its proteins. This supports separate expression, chromatin and epigenetic measurements without assuming every correlation is causal or universally inherited. [NHGRI gene expression](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Gene-Expression), [NHGRI epigenetics](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Epigenetics).

NHGRI describes genetic linkage in relation to genomic location. The catalog keeps linkage, association and causal evidence review distinct instead of turning a statistical association into an automatically actionable trait. [NHGRI linkage](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Linkage).

NIGMS describes research organisms as tools for studying life and supports multiple biological imaging technologies. That supports explicit model scope and physically distinct measurement methods rather than a single unlimited microscopy unlock. [NIGMS research organisms](https://www.nigms.nih.gov/Research/Pages/Research-Organisms), [NIGMS biomedical technology](https://www.nigms.nih.gov/about/overview/BBCB/biomedicaltechnology/Pages/default).

These sources ground selected distinctions, not all prerequisite edges, historical dates, numerical performance or clinical applications. Further technical and semantic review and historical-horizon allocation remain required.
