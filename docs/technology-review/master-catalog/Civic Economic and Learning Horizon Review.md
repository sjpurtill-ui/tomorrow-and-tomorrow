## Paid scholars, partners and research purchases

The following authored stages remain distinct and available through causal development, not calendar gates:

1. A competent traveling master or supported scholar supplies finite teaching and inquiry. Travel provisions, compensation, host capacity and practice supplies are real constraints.
2. Commissioned research defines the question, delivered evidence and acceptance limits. Patronage is an alternative to a formal modern research registry.
3. Cost-sharing records partner contributions; milestone payments release funds against agreed evidence. Neither creates missing laboratories or staff.
4. Background-knowledge terms and use licenses define access or permission. Demonstrations and transfer packages address tacit practice. Permission alone is not mastery.
5. Local reproduction and acceptance test whether delivered findings work under the contracted conditions. Failure, further adaptation and continued supplier dependence remain possible.

WIPO distinguishes formal agreements, informal knowledge transfer and background/foreground rights in its [technology-transfer overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements). Those distinctions inform these design contracts; the game does not assume a single modern legal regime across history. No research purchase imposes a law, creates legitimacy, authorizes a government's spending or grants free operating inputs.

## Mediation reconciliation

Employment Dispute Mediation duplicated the existing voluntary Mediation Practice mechanism. The retained identity includes work/pay evidence as an application and removes compulsory binding arbitration. The proposal does not guarantee agreement or uninterrupted work. WIPO's [mediation guide](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/amc/mediation/guide/index) distinguishes facilitated settlement from an arbitral decision; that mechanism distinction supports retaining mediation and arbitration separately, without counting each dispute subject as a new invention.
