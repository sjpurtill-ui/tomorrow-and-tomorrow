## Grounding and remaining review

Hydraulic generation and pumped storage use finite water, head and losses; see the US Department of Energy’s [hydropower explanation](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/water/how-hydropower-works) and [pumped-storage explanation](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/water/how-pumped-storage-hydropower-works). The draft plant boundaries are game-design proposals, not specifications derived from these introductory sources.

The separation of spacecraft structure, control, communications, thermal management, power and propulsion follows the subsystem distinctions in NASA’s [Onboard Systems](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter11-1/). Thermal and power requirements need further per-entry engineering review against NASA’s [thermal-control](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/thermal-control/) and [power](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/power-subsystems/) references. No speculative entry is asserted to have achieved a working demonstration.

Historical synonym reconciliation, additional empirical alternatives, explicit disadvantaged recovery terms and material/operating implementation remain outstanding. Recovery references in the machine-readable file indicate intended use of the existing acquisition systems; they are not newly implemented routes.
