# Medicine Interventions Depth Review

56 additional D14 proposals after four overlap exclusions. These are authored review designs, not implemented clinical services. Medicine now accounts for 189 of 280 planned identities.

## Knowledge, product and clinical service

The graph separates physical measurement, product or device qualification, and staffed clinical operation. A society can purchase a qualified product or refer to an external service without first manufacturing every component. It still pays for delivery, compatible local equipment, trained staff and follow-up. Learning a method never authorizes a treatment or guarantees benefit.

Timed cardiac stimulation, a therapeutic rhythm intervention and mechanical circulation support are distinct physical functions. Renal care can use an extracorporeal membrane or a peritoneal exchange route. Emission imaging separates tracer supply from single-photon and coincidence-detection instruments. These choices reconverge on clinical interpretation and continuing care.

## Branch review

- Ophthalmoscopic Examination: Qualified instrument findings can enter an established clinical assessment through examination or history-led inquiry; ordinary eye assessment remains available before the instrument.
- Ocular Tonometry: Qualified contact-response or optical measurement lineages can support pressure estimation; actual clinical validation is method-specific.
- Histocompatibility Typing: Serological or molecular typing can provide qualified compatibility evidence; molecular sequencing is not a universal early gate.
- Single-Photon Emission Tomography: Emission reconstruction may use shared reconstruction knowledge or independently developed qualified numerical methods; an X-ray CT scanner need not already exist.
- Positron Emission Tomography: Coincidence-emission reconstruction does not require prior construction of an X-ray CT scanner when an independent reconstruction method is demonstrated.
- Ultrasound Elastography: Measured deformation or wave-response models can ground the method; actual tissue calibration and clinical performance remain required.
- Radiology Diagnostic Display Calibration: Qualified film-viewing illumination or electronic display can be calibrated. Computerized image registration is not required before a radiograph can be viewed under measured conditions.
- Robot-Assisted Surgical Manipulation: Qualified direct endoscopic guidance can support surgeon-controlled systems without precomputed image-guided path planning.
- Ultrasonic Surgical Instruments: Qualified acoustic transducer or ultrasonic-instrument knowledge can support surgical-device development; a diagnostic imaging service is not mandatory. Clinical performance remains separately evaluated.
- Cryoablation Planning: Qualified image guidance or direct endoscopic guidance can support a defined localized intervention. Actual cooling, trained staff and evaluated clinical use remain required; diagnostic tomography is not universal.
- Thermal Ablation Planning: Qualified electrical or optical energy systems provide alternative heating architectures; method-specific dosimetry remains necessary.
- Cardiac Pacing Systems: A qualified external electronic pacing route can precede compact implanted semiconductor devices; power, electrodes and clinical limits remain real.
- Cardiac Defibrillation Systems: Different qualified energy-delivery architectures can be evaluated; a specific modern capacitor design is not a universal discovery gate.
- Organ Perfusion Preservation: Qualified organ-preservation research can precede an operating transplant service; actual recovery access, clinical indication and downstream services remain separate requirements.
- Therapeutic Monoclonal Antibodies: Locally manufactured or externally supplied qualified products can enter clinical evaluation; domestic biological manufacturing is not mandatory.
- Cell-Therapy Potency Assays: Product-function assays can be developed before an operating personalized therapy service; testing alone does not authorize clinical use.

## Outside learning and conditional outcomes

Every new destination includes five proposed routes: paid specialist instruction, partnership, commissioned evaluation, licensed or imported services, and analysis of delivered devices or authorized records. All retain competent source access, real payment or contribution, transport or transmission and finite local qualification. Imported medicine does not grant its manufacturing process; a device without staff and consumables does not supply care. Numerical acquisition costs, operational capacity and AI decisions remain pending.

Tolerance-induction research and integrated bioartificial organ support retain explicit conditional research outcomes. A promising mechanism does not establish durable benefit or useful scale. All clinical records require an evaluated indication and actual staff and supply; these descriptions deliberately remain at game-design level rather than providing clinical procedures.

## Evidence and limits

The physical distinction between single-photon and coincidence-emission imaging follows [NIBIB's nuclear-medicine explanation](https://www.nibib.nih.gov/science-education/science-topics/nuclear-medicine). The source supports those mechanisms, not the exact game dependencies or a universal clinical indication.

Distinct renal-support routes and continued treatment requirements are described by [NIDDK's kidney-failure treatment comparison](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/choosing-treatment). Game throughput and patient outcomes remain to be implemented and validated.

The distinction between electrical pacing and mechanical ventricular support is supported by [NHLBI's heart-treatment overview](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-treatments-procedures). This document makes no patient-specific treatment recommendation or claim of universal effectiveness.
