This section adds 60 D21 proposals across semiconductor processes, chip packaging, processor execution, software recovery, shared services, cooling, learning systems and robotics. At this publication checkpoint the catalog accounts for **2,161 identities: 556 implemented baseline, 48 unfinished communications definitions and 1,557 master drafts**. **2,839 remain to author.** D21 accounts for 168 of its 300 editorial target, leaving 132.

These are authored mechanisms and operating contracts, not newly working fabrication plants, processors, networks or AI. The broad catalog must still be completed and connected to actual simulation behavior.

## Distinct mechanisms

The scope reconciliation file separates broad integration knowledge from specific physical or computational mechanisms. An integrated-circuit concept does not supply every implantation, etching or joining process. A processor architecture does not automatically include speculative or parallel execution. Transactional consistency may be implemented through different logging and version rules; those mechanisms are not all forced onto every database.

Manufacturing, ownership and knowledge remain separate. Purchased dies can support a locally qualified package assembly without domestic wafer production. A licensed compute service offers finite work through actual communication and paid capacity. Neither purchase reveals every manufacturing process, supplies free electricity, removes heat or grants mastery of a provider's model training.

## Outside learning and operating consequences

Every record specifies a paid scholar route, reciprocal partnership, commissioned research, licensed/imported service and specimen or program examination. Their common costs are source availability, compensation, actual delivery, finite local adaptation and continuing operating inputs. An opaque cloud service is not an inspectable chip or training process. A successful imported computation is evidence of a delivered result, not of domestic fabrication mastery.

A society with packaged processors and suitable power can rent additional remote compute capacity. Access still consumes supplier availability, a functioning communications path, payment and data-transfer time. Losing the supplier ends the dependent service after whatever local buffering remains. Domestic wafer production is a separate investment, and local compute still needs installed equipment, cooling, maintenance and operators.

Server cooling removes actual heat through qualified air or liquid paths. Water and electrical use are measured separately; workload scheduling cannot claim unlimited performance from an efficiency discovery. Improved query plans, compressed models and accelerators only benefit suitable workloads, with measured overhead, error or quality tradeoffs. Model confidence is evaluated evidence rather than a truth bonus.

Runtime recipes, compatible component inventories, physical installations, finite compute/heat/utility dispatch, paid acquisition penalties, AI decisions and saved service state remain unfinished. General-led military use must consume these actual services through the existing command system rather than create direct player control of machines or units.

## Evidence grounding

Intel describes die assembly and testing as a distinct part of chip manufacture. This supports separating wafer processing, die testing, interconnection and packaging instead of assigning a single unlimited semiconductor unlock. [Intel chip assembly and testing](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/tech101/manufacturing/how-silicon-die-become-chip-packages.html).

The Department of Energy's data-center design guide treats IT equipment, environmental conditions, airflow, cooling, electrical systems and heat recovery together. These are operating burdens that computing demand must actually pay in the game. [DOE data-center design guide](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/articles/best-practices-guide-energy-efficient-data-center-design).

NIST's AI framework addresses design, development, use and evaluation, including continued measurement and response. The learning-system proposals therefore retain evaluated task scope, uncertainty and monitored operation rather than making model training a guaranteed correct decision-maker. [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).

These sources support selected distinctions, not every proposed edge, historical date, claimed deployment maturity or numerical performance. Broader technical and semantic review remains necessary. Frontier applications in earlier sections retain their explicit uncertainty; these engineering methods do not establish guaranteed general intelligence or useful fault-tolerant quantum computation.
