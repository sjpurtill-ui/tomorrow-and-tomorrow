## Decisions that matter

Hydraulic mills, sails and angular sky records belong to earlier learned practice. Industrial heat, steam, electric-machine regulation and generator synchronization are classified with their physical plant lineage. Empirical fuel-moisture comparisons retain their earlier route rather than requiring modern calorimetry.

Modern space exploration does not become futuristic because the subject name contains orbit, habitat or nuclear. Orbital assembly is demonstrated engineering, as NASA's [station assembly record](https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/international-space-station-assembly-elements/) shows. NASA's [space nuclear history](https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/space-nuclear-propulsion/) records the SNAP-10A fission reactor in space in 1965. These references support classifying the existing mechanisms as modern; they do not certify every operating design here.

Cryogenic orbital depot integration is a different scope from tank tests, transfer plumbing or a conventional propellant transfer. The authored node requires maintained installed storage, real deliveries, gauging and thermal support. NASA describes [cryogenic storage demonstrations](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/tech-demo-missions-program/cryogenic-fluid-management-cfm/stay-cool-nasa-tests-innovative-technique-for-super-cold-fuel-storage/) as technology development. This supports keeping experimental components separate from the proposed sustained depot service; no projected mission success is assumed.

Fusion confinement and representative component/tooling trials stay modern research. The net-power integration node, sustained fuel breeding/inventory and plant blanket functions have distinct advanced operating claims. Qualified research is not free electricity, infinite fuel or an established component lifetime.

Interstellar probe navigation, reusable extraterrestrial surface transport and asteroid material processing receive advanced assignments for the stated operational scope. This does not assert successful operation. A small sample return is not bulk asteroid processing; a one-time ascent is not a maintained surface-to-orbit transport service. Ground analog research and earlier component mechanisms remain available independently.
