# Water and Ecology Depth Review

78 additional authored proposals: 40 water mechanisms and 38 ecology mechanisms, after three overlap exclusions and one added seed-dispersal mechanism. These are designs for review, not implemented services. D02 now accounts for 115 of 180 planned identities; D03 accounts for 105 of 180.

## Physical limits and branching

Water supply must account for source withdrawal, travel, storage change, treatment losses and actual delivered quality. A pump or new aquifer model does not create recharge. Contaminant removal produces spent media, brine, sludge or off-gas that still needs handling. De-watering is not disinfection, and clear-looking water is not proof of safe delivery.

Ecology models separate observation, inference and intervention. A dispersal model can estimate that propagules might arrive; it does not establish survival. Grazing rotation changes timing and access, not automatically carrying capacity. Restoration remains limited by the actual site, viable organisms, water, time and competing uses. Learning never imposes a protected area or reallocates civic labor.

Early empirical routes remain possible: observed high water before a formal discharge curve, practical forage monitoring before formal plots, different local construction materials and several membrane lineages. Supplied exchange media do not require domestic resin manufacture. A purchased field service remains bounded by provider access and capacity.

## Alternative route review

- River Diversion Weirs: Locally qualified stone or timber works can guide a diversion; concrete is not mandatory.
- Earthen Flood Embankments: Practical compaction knowledge or characterized soil response can support earthen works; modern computation is not mandatory.
- Flood Spillway Design: Empirical high-water observations can precede formal discharge ratings, with wider uncertainty and bounded accepted risk.
- Hydraulic Jump Dissipation: Measured head and observed channel response can precede more formal hydraulic explanations.
- Bank-Filtration Intakes: Qualified bank behavior may be established through a flow model or observed travel evidence; treatment performance still requires monitoring.
- Granular Carbon Adsorption: Suitable porous carbon may come from different carbonization lineages; ordinary charcoal is not automatically qualified adsorption media.
- Water Ion Exchange: A qualified naturally occurring exchange medium or a supplied manufactured resin can support the method. Domestic polymer synthesis and mineral-classification theory are not universal gates; actual exchange performance and regeneration or replacement supply must be demonstrated.
- Membrane Integrity Testing: Integrity methods must match the installed membrane and its declared barrier; one physical test is not valid for every membrane class.
- Membrane Fouling Control: Fouling diagnosis can develop for any qualified membrane lineage; cleaning must remain compatible with the actual material.
- Sludge Mechanical Dewatering: Collected sludge can be dewatered before or after biological stabilization; dewatering alone does not establish sanitary quality.
- Species Interaction Networks: Animal-sign observations or plant-community observations can ground an early interaction model; laboratory genetics is not mandatory.
- Population Resource-Limit Models: Qualified local abundance and resource observations can precede formal imperfect-detection models; confidence follows evidence quality.
- Lake Stratification Models: Measured temperature and density-related buoyancy ground stratification; water turbidity is not a causal prerequisite.
- Migratory Connectivity Tracking: Repeated physical signs or identified encounters can connect seasonal sites before electronic tracking.
- Wildlife Crossing Structures: Qualified overpasses or underpasses may use different civil-engineering lineages; geometry must suit the target organisms.
- Rangeland Residual-Forage Management: Repeated practical forage observations can guide pressure before formal plot sampling; estimates retain local uncertainty.
- Seagrass Establishment Methods: Seed viability knowledge or vegetative propagation experience can support a qualified establishment trial. The selected marine species needs demonstrated suitable propagules and substrate; a terrestrial root crop cannot substitute for seagrass.
- Coral Recruitment Support: Recruitment response can be evaluated through identified individuals or qualified sampled abundance; marking itself is not universally required.
- Bioacoustic Species Monitoring: Recorded calls can support qualified monitoring before modern automated classification; actual recording fidelity still matters.
- Restoration Genetic Diversity Design: Empirical source-response evidence can guide early source mixtures before molecular genetics; unmeasured mixing risks remain explicit.
- Seed Dispersal Models: Observed carrier activity or formal detection models can support dispersal inference; viable arrival remains distinct from successful establishment.

## Disadvantaged recovery

Each destination specifies five proposed outside-learning contracts: paid scholar instruction, reciprocal partnership, commissioned analysis, licensed or imported services, and study of delivered hardware, samples or records. Contracts retain actual source competence, payment or contribution, travel or transmission and local demonstration. Staff and instruments cannot serve unlimited settlements at once. A provider's favorable result does not establish that a different local site will respond the same way. Numerical costs, delays and operating integration remain pending.

## Evidence and limits

Selective adsorption, ion exchange, membranes and aeration have different treatment functions and residuals. [EPA's treatment-technology overview](https://www.epa.gov/sdwa/overview-drinking-water-treatment-technologies) supports these distinctions; it does not validate a universal treatment chain or these game prerequisite edges.

Groundwater withdrawal can couple pressure change to sediment compaction and subsidence. [USGS's aquifer-compaction explanation](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/land-subsidence-in-california/science/aquifer-compaction-due-groundwater-pumping) supports the distinction between a pumping response and potentially irreversible storage deformation.

Marine restoration depends on actual substrate, recruitment and site conditions. [NOAA's oyster habitat account](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/habitat-conservation/oyster-reef-habitat) illustrates this dependency; it does not establish that one restoration intervention transfers across oyster, coral, kelp and seagrass systems. Proposed ecological models still require historical and scientific review at their actual scope.
