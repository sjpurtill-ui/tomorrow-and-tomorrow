# Economy and Research Finance Depth Review

56 additional authored proposals, after four overlap exclusions. These are review designs, not implemented discoveries. D17 now accounts for 123 of its 200 planned identities.

The section connects early exchange and patronage to monetary accounting, production flows, labor agreements, research acquisition and future service dependence. Learning a method does not enact a monetary regime, impose an employment arrangement or authorize an autonomous buyer. Existing civic authorities and actual actors retain decisions and labor ownership. No calendar or era gate is introduced.

## Branching and operating rules

Empirical and customary approaches remain available before formal institutions: accepted commodities can arise from reciprocal obligations; accounts need not use coins; confidential offers need not use paper; mutual loss pools need not know actuarial theory. A society can obtain external research through a supported scholar before it develops procurement offices. Formal methods can improve coordination without retroactively gating earlier practice.

Contracts represent claims on actual people, goods and capacity. Credit can create offsetting bank assets and deposit liabilities; it cannot create grain, tools or settlement liquidity. Auctions need capable counterparties and delivered lots. Insurance can exhaust reserves. Every form of research acquisition still needs an available competent source, paid access, real travel or data delivery and local interpretation or demonstration.

## Alternative route review

- Commodity Money Conventions: Acceptance can grow through exchange equivalences or reciprocal obligations; formal monetary institutions are not required.
- Unit of Account Conventions: A shared valuation unit can emerge from trading or debt reckoning; coined money is not mandatory.
- Monetary Piece Stamping: Suitable struck or cast blanks can support a marked monetary piece; metal supply and mint quality remain physical requirements.
- Redeemable Payment Notes: Transferable redemption claims may use authenticated written or encoded tokens; industrial printing is not mandatory.
- Payment Token Authentication: Authentication can develop for marked metal or documentary payment claims; the chosen security feature must match the medium.
- Sealed Bid Auctions: Offers can be privately encoded before advanced writing or electronic systems; trustworthy secrecy and a closing rule remain necessary.
- Credit Underwriting: Qualified community repayment evidence can precede corporate accounting; limited evidence produces wider uncertainty, not free certainty.
- Insurance Risk Pooling: Customary mutual contributions or formal capital pools can share specified losses; actuarial theory is an improvement, not a first-use gate.
- Wage and Time Accounts: Recorded service periods or agreed market compensation support work accounts; payment can be in kind and does not require banks.
- Piecework Compensation: Accepted countable work can be judged by graded goods or demonstrated craft standards; quality must remain verifiable.
- Research Patronage Agreements: Patronage can support practical demonstrations or worked reasoning examples before universities, written histories and procurement offices.
- Commissioned Research Scoping: An explicitly scoped paid question can evolve from patronage as well as formal research administration.
- Technology Use Licenses: A negotiated permission can rely on craft arrangements or formal commercial disclosure; a universal patent system is not mandatory.
- Know-How Transfer Packages: Transfer can use practical craft examples or recorded experimental methods; a license alone is not know-how.
- Scarcity Allocation Mechanism Trials: Empirical comparison can precede formal optimization; measured outcomes and rights constraints still require review.

## Research acquisition consequences

Scholar patronage supports inquiry and instruction through real subsistence, trade or payment. A commission buys a defined investigation, with uncertainty disclosed; a failed hypothesis can still be an honest completed deliverable. Milestone payments require actual reviewed work. A partnership records each party's staff, equipment, specimens or financial contribution and defines access to earlier knowledge.

A technology license permits a bounded use; it is not a knowledge grant. A know-how package can lower reconstruction effort but leaves equipment, practice and qualification costs. Reproduction acceptance distinguishes the delivered report from a locally usable capability. Imported services retain provider capacity, delivery, fees and interruption risks. Recovery contracts in the JSON specify all five routes for each destination: scholar envoy, partnership, purchased research, licensed/imported service and reverse engineering. They remain design proposals; numerical penalties and operating validation are pending.

## Evidence and limits

The distinction between licensing, assignment, collaboration and practical knowledge transfer is supported by [WIPO technology-transfer agreements](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements). The proposed game routes and penalties are design decisions, not a claim that one legal system applies throughout history.

The treatment of bank lending as creation of corresponding loan assets and deposit liabilities follows the [Bank of England explanation of money creation](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy). This describes a monetary mechanism, not a universal model of all banks or a license to create physical resources.

Information, allocation and bidding rules distinguish auction mechanisms; the [2020 Nobel scientific background](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2020/09/advanced-economicsciencesprize2020.pdf) supports those distinctions. It does not validate these exact prerequisite edges or guarantee competitive outcomes in the simulation.
