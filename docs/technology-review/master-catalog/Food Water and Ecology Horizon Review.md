# Food, Water and Ecology Horizon and Branch Review

This review classifies 314 existing discoveries across food, water and ecology. It also removes one duplicated grazing identity and repairs 20 unnecessary prerequisite contracts. The complete catalog now accounts for **3,030 distinct identities**, with **1,970 still to author** toward 5,000. The source inventory comprises 556 baseline records, 48 unfinished communications records and 2,426 master-draft records. Three food identities now also have a separately verified canonical implementation; these overlapping records do not add another three identities.

The 21 advanced assignments elsewhere remain a partial count, not the full tree's future total.

Modern research and production are not hypothetical just because economic scale remains uncertain. FDA's [cultured-cell food material](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-ingredients-packaging/human-food-made-cultured-animal-cells) records an initial completed consultation in 2022. That supports treating the basic evaluated cell-food mechanism as modern; it does not establish every proposed structure, scale or game operating model. True frontier food systems still require distinct authored mechanisms and demonstrated operating limits.

## Duplicate reconciliation

Rotational Grazing Design was merged into Rotational Grazing Plans. Both moved herds between areas according to rest and forage recovery. The retained identity now includes actual herd, land, water, staff and recovery constraints plus all five disadvantaged outside-learning terms. It cannot create land rights or guarantee higher carrying capacity. FAO's [rotational grazing definition](https://agrovoc.fao.org/browse/agrovoc/en/page/c_6663) describes this spatial movement practice; it does not justify counting the two labels separately.

The retained node requires animal taming plus either managed fallow or residual-forage management. Selective breeding is not a necessary foundation. Residual-forage assessment remains distinct: it adjusts stocking pressure; spatial rotation determines where and when herds graze.

## Repaired causal paths

A later editorial horizon on a parent is a review signal, not an automatic error. Each repair below was evaluated for a concrete unnecessary gate. Dates and horizon labels do not change runtime discovery permissions. Actual operating inputs remain required.
