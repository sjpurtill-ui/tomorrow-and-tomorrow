# Medicine, biology and computation

181 authored draft discoveries: 60 medicine, 61 biology and 60 computation. This section connects empirical care and natural history to molecular biology, modern clinical services, programmable computing and conditional future research. It is a review document, not an implemented game delivery. No date, era or civilization identity is an unlock requirement.

## What the player gains

Care advances from recognizing and responding to individual injuries toward organized services with finite beds, staff, products and transport. Biology advances from comparing organisms to understanding cells, inheritance and qualified biological production. Computing advances from repeatable methods and physical circuits toward software systems, inference, robotics and specialized hardware. Knowing a discovery permits the relevant learning and operating choices; it does not conjure facilities, manufacture, people, products or reliable outcomes.

The section adds 26 explicit alternative prerequisite groups. Shared foundations are all required; within each alternative group, one foundation suffices. If several alternative groups are shown, each group must be satisfied. All routes reconverge on the same discovery identity.

## Important branch corrections

- Natural selection can be inferred through observation and breeding before mathematical population genetics. Whole-organism developmental observation can precede cell observation.
- Routine clinical observation, fracture support and newborn thermal care retain empirical routes. Hospitals and precision thermometers improve specific capabilities without being compulsory origins for every care practice.
- Clinical hand hygiene does not require germ theory. This preserves an evidence-led route instead of forcing effective practice to wait for a later explanation.
- Blood compatibility can be investigated before a comprehensive immune model. Biological reference materials do not require industrial bioproduct manufacture.
- Sequence readout can use different physical detection methods; an electronic computer is not a compulsory foundation for the first manually read sequence.
- An integrated circuit may use bipolar or MOS devices. File organization does not depend on packet networking. A simple listening tube does not depend on telephone components.
- Imported qualified medicines can support stewardship and immunization services. A remote clinical consultation can use a voice link; digital modems are optional.
- Renal replacement requires qualified membrane exchange and fluid control, not ordinary water microfiltration. Semiconductor packaging depends on relevant thermal and mechanical knowledge, not food-retort validation.

These corrections are design judgments informed by the sources below; the whole graph still needs wider historical and semantic review.

## Acquiring knowledge from other civilizations

Every record identifies eligible outside methods, evidence, a local demonstration and the same destination identity. These are proposed contracts; costs, work coefficients and runtime mappings are not implemented in this document.

| Method | What must actually exist | Disadvantage and continuing local work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Following another civilization's research | A competent source and accessible, attributable observations or teaching material | Wait for evidence to exist and arrive, interpret its gaps, and repeat the local demonstration. An imported report is not mastery. This is evidence acquisition within the teaching or partnership route, not another technology identity. |
| Scholar envoy | A source able to teach this subject, an available scholar, an actual journey and a hosting agreement | Pay or trade for travel, subsistence and teaching time. Local learners consume finite time. Departure, war or lost access can halt teaching; completed local knowledge remains. |
| Research partnership | Both parties contribute agreed evidence, personnel, equipment access or work | Pay the reciprocal commitment, wait for exchanged material and validate foreign results against local conditions. Neither party receives the other's entire catalog. |
| Commissioned research | A supplier capable of the requested work, an enforceable agreement and funded research capacity | Pay for outside work and accept delivery and supplier dependence. This can shorten elapsed research time when capacity is purchased, but local foundations and adaptation remain. Payment alone does not guarantee a successful discovery. |
| Licensed design | A usable design or program, permitted access and a competent local team | Pay negotiated fees and adapt it to actual equipment. Access to instructions does not create fabrication capacity. Continued access may depend on the agreement; already learned knowledge is not retroactively erased. |
| Reverse engineering | An acquired physical device suitable for inspection and a competent local analysis team | Pay acquisition, transport and analysis; inspection may consume or damage the specimen. Hidden process knowledge remains uncertain until locally demonstrated. This route is assigned to hardware here, not indiscriminately to patient care or biological material. |

Clinical learning uses supervised practice and de-identified records. Biological learning uses documented observations and appropriately handled reference material. Hardware learning uses designs and measured devices. Software learning uses inspectable methods, programs, models and evaluation cases. The JSON records specify the matching profile, eligible methods, the local demonstration and continuing operating requirements for each discovery.

Purchased operation and purchased research are separate. Renting a computation or clinical service consumes the supplier's actual staffed capacity and a functioning connection or journey. It can provide a result while leaving the buyer dependent on the supplier. It does not imply that the buyer can reproduce the equipment, process or knowledge locally.

## Reading the catalog

Each row states a distinct proposed mechanism, its intended observable result and the physical or organizational conditions needed to operate it. Separate capabilities can depend on the same equipment without being duplicates. Conversely, a new label is not evidence of a new mechanism. Comparison with existing care, field evacuation, microscopy, machine-control and government records informed this section; full semantic reconciliation remains open.

## Grounding and limits

WHO's historical review describes infection-control practice based on observed transmission before later microbiological explanations. That supports retaining an empirical hand-hygiene route. [WHO, Historical perspective on hand hygiene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144018/).

NHGRI's account of Darwin's 1859 publication supports a natural-selection branch that does not wait for later molecular or population-genetic models. Its sequencing history describes different detection approaches, supporting multiple instrument routes. [NHGRI, Darwin and natural selection](https://www.genome.gov/25520157/online-education-kit-1859-darwin-published-on-the-origin-of-species-proposing-continual-evolution-of-species); [NHGRI, DNA sequencing](https://www.genome.gov/25520304/online-education-kit-197577-dna-sequencing).

NIST describes measured computing services in terms of metered resources. The proposed purchased-compute branch therefore accounts for actual processing, storage and communication capacity. [NIST, Measured service](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/Measured_service).

IBM's educational treatment distinguishes physical errors, encoded information and fault-tolerant operations. Quantum research here remains conditional on characterized devices and correction resources; it gives no universal speed bonus. [IBM, Fault-tolerant quantum computation](https://learning.quantum.ibm.com/course/foundations-of-quantum-error-correction/fault-tolerant-quantum-computation).

These references ground selected distinctions; they do not validate every prerequisite, clinical outcome or proposed future capability. Future interventions require demonstrated outcomes within the game's modeled conditions. Genetic editing, regeneration, autonomous experiments and quantum algorithms are not automatic cures or guaranteed breakthroughs.
