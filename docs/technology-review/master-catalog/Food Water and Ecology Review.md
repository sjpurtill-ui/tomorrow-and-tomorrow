# Food, water and ecology

179 authored drafts spanning food production, water services and ecological stewardship. They state proposed mechanisms, shared and alternative foundations, real operating requirements and conditional consequences. These are not implemented capabilities or treatment instructions.

The draft well-curb entry was removed because the existing Protected Wellheads discovery already includes raised rims and drainage. Edible-Oil Clarification is separate from existing seed-oil pressing because it removes suspended residues and entrained moisture after expression. Further semantic review remains necessary.

## Grounding and remaining review

Water technologies must address particular contaminants and operating limits, rather than treating clear water or one device as universally safe. The EPA provides an [overview of drinking-water treatment technologies](https://www.epa.gov/sdwa/overview-drinking-water-treatment-technologies). The game entries describe separate treatment and verification capacities; they are not plant specifications.

Soil disturbance, ground cover and crop diversity interact rather than granting independent unlimited bonuses. See FAO’s [conservation agriculture principles](https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture). Existing live residue-cover and reduced-tillage discoveries are retained instead of being recounted under new names.

Aquaponics joins aquatic production and soilless crops through managed water and nutrient flows, with water-quality and system-design requirements described in FAO’s [aquaponic food production reference](https://www.fao.org/family-farming/detail/en/c/1743023/). Proposed closed-habitat entries extend these dependencies as game-design questions; self-sufficiency is not assumed.

Historical prerequisites, regional alternatives, biological feasibility, numeric balances, explicit disadvantaged recovery routes and actual operating implementation remain pending. Unique IDs and reachable edges establish neither scientific validation nor completion.

Current prerequisite corrections are documented in [Food, Water and Ecology Horizon and Branch Review](Food%20Water%20and%20Ecology%20Horizon%20Review.md); its before/after table supersedes affected prerequisite cells in this publication checkpoint. Current JSON/TSV definitions are authoritative.
