# Conduit manufacturing and service review

Ten new authored discoveries resolve a specific missing physical chain. These are proposals for review, not operating runtime discoveries. The current ledger contains 614 implemented discoveries and 2,424 drafts: 3,038 distinct identities toward 5,000. Construction and food handoffs awaiting integration are already represented identities and must not inflate this total.

| Discovery | Required foundations | Alternative foundations | Intended player consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clay Pipe Forming | clay_shaping, clay_tempering | None | Produces unfired conduit sections; malformed or cracked sections consume labor and clay without usable installed capacity. |
| Ceramic Pipe Firing Qualification | clay_pipe_forming, kiln_control | None | Releases a bounded batch of fired pipe for suitable service; poor firing raises rejection and repair demand. Early fired clay does not automatically gain modern vitrified-pipe performance. |
| Ceramic Pipe Fit Gauges | clay_pipe_forming, standard_measures | None | Sorts compatible sections and rejects bad fits before crews spend transport and trench labor; it grants neither strength nor pressure certification. |
| Clay Pipe Socket Jointing | ceramic_pipe_firing_qualification | lime_mortar OR puddled_clay_water_linings | Joins finite gravity-conduit lengths with retained leakage and movement limits; a clay seal remains available without lime technology. |
| Rigid Pipe Bedding | drainage | ceramic_pipe_firing_qualification OR wooden_log_conduits | Reduces unsupported spans and installation breakage for the treated length, at material and labor cost; poor ground can still settle. |
| Buried Pipe Load Assessment | rigid_pipe_bedding, standard_measures | None | Restricts feasible burial depths and traffic crossings; weak sections require stronger supply, better support or rerouting instead of a universal durability bonus. |
| Conduit Infiltration Testing | standard_measures | clay_pipe_socket_jointing OR wooden_log_conduits OR pressure_pipe_jointing | Locates sections needing further investigation and prevents untested sections being treated as tight; consumes inspection time without automatically locating every individual defect. |
| Wooden Log Conduits | joinery | wheel_hub_boring OR cylinder_boring | Supplies a locally feasible conduit route where timber and boring skill exist; bore size, leakage and timber condition limit service. |
| Gravity Conduit Grade Control | geometric_survey, drainage | None | Makes chosen gravity flow paths feasible and exposes reverse grades before burial; terrain can demand extra excavation or another route. |
| Sewer Rodding Service | covered_sewers | joinery OR wire_drawing | Restores part of obstructed service through finite maintenance work; inaccessible or structurally failed sections require excavation or replacement. |

The four ceramic manufacturing records distinguish hollow forming, usable fired batches, compatible dimensions and installed gravity joints. Existing kiln control, shared measures, pressure jointing and drainage retain their identities. Bedding, loading assessment, section leakage checks, grading and rodding address different installation or service failures. Bored timber is an independent material route. None supplies clean drinking water merely because a pipe exists.

Every record includes finite local demonstration and acquisition costs. Paid scholars and purchased research deliver instruction or evidence; partnerships require contributions. Imported pipe or installation service can complete bounded work without unlocking local manufacture. Material inspection cannot expose every hidden process parameter. No route creates stock, labor or mastery.

The historical-horizon assignments are editorial scope labels, not dates or unlock gates. The early fired-clay proposal intentionally does not inherit modern vitrified pipe properties. The AND/OR checker verifies identity and reachability; it does not prove chronological accuracy, semantic uniqueness, physical implementation or viable campaign pacing.

Source evidence and its limits:

- The [National Park Service account of Mammoth Cave saltpetre works](https://www.nps.gov/places/saltpetre-vats-rotunda.htm) documents joined hollow wooden logs carrying water. This supports a timber route; the proposed tool prerequisites and game costs are design judgments.
- The [NCPI engineering manual](https://www.ncpi.org/assets/ncpi-engineering-manual.pdf) separates pipe manufacture, installation, testing and service. It supports treating these as different physical concerns; the game records are not construction instructions.
- [ASTM C301](https://store.astm.org/c0301-18r22.html) concerns testing vitrified clay pipe before installation. Material acceptance is therefore kept separate from installed-section checks.
- [NCPI’s FAQ](https://www.ncpi.org/frequently-asked-questions/) distinguishes older clay pipe from modern manufacturing and joint systems. Its broad durability claims are not used as automatic game bonuses.
