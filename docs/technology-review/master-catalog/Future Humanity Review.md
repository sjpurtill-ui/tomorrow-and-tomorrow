# Future Humanity Review

This section proposes 56 distinct D24 discoveries after excluding four semantic duplicates. They connect earlier fields into supported human futures rather than requiring every civilization to pursue one endpoint. These are review drafts, not installed game capabilities. At this publication checkpoint, the master contains 1,437 drafts plus 556 implemented baseline identities and 48 unfinished communications identities: **2,041 accounted identities; 2,959 remain to author**.

## What research completion means

A discovery records an evidenced method or a bounded investigation result. An uncertain program can complete with a negative result, a constrained operating envelope or a revised design. That evidence remains useful and visible. Research completion does not silently create a healthy population, functioning habitat, available organ, ecological restoration or successful deflection. Deployment separately needs qualified evidence and real operating resources. No era, Earth date, political regime or fictional calendar year is an unlock gate.

The JSON records carry a proposed confidence class, an unresolved limit, completion-evidence contract and five outside-learning routes. Confidence describes the stated mechanism; it is not an assertion that a complete settlement-scale application is demonstrated. These editorial classifications still require wider source review. The grounded default excludes guaranteed mind uploading, faster-than-light travel, infinite resources and guaranteed human reproductive viability outside Earth-like conditions.

Proposed classifications: 24 demonstrated capability, 22 demonstrated components, 10 plausible extrapolation.

## Branching and disadvantaged recovery

Fourteen alternative foundation groups preserve routes such as local records instead of electronic telemedicine, public budgeting instead of private project finance, and community support instead of public trusts. Learning a procedure does not force its political adoption. These are knowledge relationships: buildings, software, clinicians, physical stocks and equipment remain independent operating requirements. Alternative foundations do not waive those requirements.

Each discovery offers a proposed paid scholar route, reciprocal partnership, purchased research, licensed or imported service, and artifact/evidence examination where applicable. Every route requires a competent available source, real delivery time, payment or contribution, and local qualification. An artifact cannot independently prove lifetime health outcomes or transfer tacit institutional practice. A research supplier cannot sell a demonstrated result it does not possess. Service imports retain ongoing dependence and do not grant mastery. Runtime coefficients, source selection, declining returns, delivery enforcement and AI decisions remain to implement and balance.

## Evidence grounding and limits

NASA separates radiation, isolation, distance, gravity and hostile environments as human-spaceflight hazards. That supports treating habitat viability as several unresolved constraints, rather than interpreting life-support hardware as proof of safe lifelong habitation. [NASA human-spaceflight risks](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/). Its artificial-gravity evidence report supports investigating human responses separately from calculating rotating structures. [NASA artificial-gravity evidence report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150009486).

DART demonstrated a particular kinetic-impact deflection. It does not prove that every body can be diverted at any warning time; this section separates orbit uncertainty, accountable mission choice, intervention design and measured outcome. [NASA DART](https://science.nasa.gov/planetary-defense-dart/).

NOAA describes radiation effects on spacecraft systems, supporting explicit monitoring and protection requirements without making shelter an immunity upgrade. [NOAA space-weather effects](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/our-environment/space-weather/the-effects-of-space-weather-earth).

These sources support selected distinctions, not all 56 prerequisites, clinical applications, numerical performance claims or maturity classifications. This document specifies game research and resource behavior rather than medical treatment instructions. Broader historical and technical review remains outstanding.
