# Energy Systems Depth Review

This section adds 58 D05 proposals after removing two proposals already covered by existing discoveries. They deepen fuels, thermal machinery, renewable systems, storage, electricity networks and future plant maintenance. At this publication checkpoint the catalog accounts for **2,219 identities: 556 implemented baseline, 48 pending communications and 1,615 master drafts**. **2,781 remain to author.** Energy accounts for 137 of its 260 editorial target, leaving 123.

These are authored proposals, not operating plants or newly delivered electricity. Every listed output depends on actual equipment, feedstock, environmental conditions, staff and maintenance. Imported fuel, power or specialist operation remains an ongoing dependency.

## Distinct mechanisms and boundaries

Fuel Heat Value Testing was excluded as an application of Calorimetry. Fusion Net Electric Demonstration was excluded as an acceptance requirement of Fusion Power Integration. Both remain needed behavior or evidence, but add no discovery identity. The scope reconciliation records these exclusions and requires measured net electrical delivery after all declared plant consumption before a fusion plant receives a net-generation capability.

Storage power and stored energy are separate quantities. A powerful nearly empty store cannot cover a long supply deficit. Charging consumes actual energy; discharge, standby, conversion and degradation losses remain physical costs. Dispatch and demand-response knowledge change timing and allocation, not conservation laws.

Heat recovery can supply a suitable nearby demand only when useful heat exists at an appropriate condition. Recovered heat is not counted twice as independent generated energy. Air and liquid cooling consume equipment and operating resources; water savings may trade against electrical work or weather-dependent output.

## Outside research and ongoing dependence

All records propose five recovery methods: a paid visiting practitioner, reciprocal partnership, purchased engineering study, licensed/imported service and physical component or operating-evidence examination. Each requires a competent source, payment or contribution, actual delivery, local learning and continuing inputs. These contracts remain proposals; numeric work penalties, dispatch enforcement and AI investment are not implemented by this document.

For example, a society may import electricity over a functioning interconnector before building its own generator of the supplier's type. The received service is bounded by supplier generation, line capacity, losses, timing and payment. Studying delivered operating records can support local research, but ongoing electricity purchases do not automatically grant turbine or reactor mastery.

A society may learn storage dispatch through pumped water, battery-state measurement or thermal accounting. The actual installed storage medium still determines power, duration, efficiency and degradation. A purchased battery does not confer every chemistry or pack-production method; it requires compatible protection, maintenance and eventually replacement.

Geothermal field trials retain site-specific geological uncertainty. Fusion component life and remote maintenance retain uncertain integrated performance. Completing a research question can narrow the feasible operating envelope or establish that a proposed design is unsuitable; it does not produce a guaranteed profitable plant. Long-term resource and service models remain part of the required implementation.

## Evidence grounding

The Department of Energy explicitly distinguishes storage energy capacity from power capacity. This supports separate duration and dispatch accounting rather than a single storage-strength bonus. [DOE solar and storage basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-solar-energy-and-storage-basics).

DOE describes enhanced geothermal development as creating usable reservoir flow where natural conditions are insufficient and identifies induced seismicity among environmental issues requiring assessment. That supports separating reservoir development, reinjection and monitored operating response. [DOE enhanced geothermal systems](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/geothermal/enhanced-geothermal-systems), [DOE environmental analysis](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/geothermal/environmental-analysis).

ITER explains that its generated heat is removed rather than converted into delivered electricity. Its scientific demonstration is not proof of an electricity-producing commercial plant. The retained Fusion Power Integration identity therefore requires whole-plant electrical accounting and measured operation. [ITER heat-to-electricity distinction](https://www.iter.org/node/20687/turning-neutrons-electricity).

These sources support selected distinctions, not all prerequisite edges, maturity assignments, process economics or historical dates. Wider technical and semantic review and the separate historical-horizon allocation remain outstanding.
