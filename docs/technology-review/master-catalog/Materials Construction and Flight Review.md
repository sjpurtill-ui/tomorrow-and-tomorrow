# Materials, construction and flight

180 authored drafts: 60 materials, 60 construction and 60 aviation discoveries. They connect material properties and production to qualified structures, aircraft and operating services. This is a proposed catalog for review, not a delivery of implemented factories, buildings or aircraft.

The section adds 19 explicit alternative prerequisite groups. Shared foundations are all required; one entry from each alternative group is required. Dates, global eras and civilization identities do not unlock discoveries. A qualified alternative material or mechanism changes costs and possibilities; it does not remove a physical requirement.

## What changes for the civilization

Materials provide different combinations of strength, weight, corrosion behavior, temperature limits, process burden and recoverability. Acquiring a sample can show a useful property without supplying the process that makes it. Qualification belongs to actual feedstocks and production conditions, not merely to a material's name.

Construction combines ground, structure, enclosure and operating services. A frame, an occupied building and a maintained long-lived asset are different states. Better materials do not supply foundations, installation crews, water, ventilation, inspection or upkeep. Building form remains a choice shaped by climate, local resources, purpose and labor.

Aviation combines lifting or buoyant support, structure, control, propulsion where required, crew competence and ground services. An aircraft's nominal speed does not establish its payload, range, weather envelope, serviceability or usable airports. The tree retains unpowered and lighter-than-air routes as well as different powered aircraft families.

| Capability choice | Different means | Requirements that remain |

| Extend service life | Inspection, repair, replacement, reuse and maintained records | People, compatible parts, funds and actual work; records alone do not repair anything |

## Outside learning and dependence

Each record identifies appropriate evidence, a local demonstration, continuing operating requirements and the costs of outside assistance. These recovery contracts are proposals; numerical work, price, failure and interruption rules remain to be implemented and balanced.

A civilization can rebuild missing foundations, receive delayed foreign research, hire a visiting specialist for payment or trade, contribute to a partnership, commission a defined study, or license applicable knowledge. It needs an actually competent source and real travel or delivery. Local workers must reproduce the relevant result under local conditions. Buying additional research capacity can reduce elapsed time while creating financial and supplier dependence.

Imported materials, building services or flights can bridge a practical shortage without granting knowledge of their production. A licensed aircraft plan does not provide an airframe or trained crew. A delivered aluminum component does not provide an electrolytic plant or its electricity. A foreign builder can erect a structure only through actual labor and supplies, and local maintenance capability may still be missing afterward.

Specimen study applies where an actual sample or device can provide relevant evidence. It may consume or damage that specimen and leave hidden processing information unresolved. Not every practice is learned by dismantling an object. All paths reconverge on the same identity rather than stacking discoveries for different sources.

## Branch corrections retained in this draft

- Brass has an ore-based learning route that need not wait for isolated metallic zinc.
- Empirical adhesive trials and plant-product knowledge can precede formal elasticity or modern biological classification.
- Composite laminates can use different qualified reinforcements and matrices. Glass fibers need not be learned through hollow-tube manufacture.
- Technical ceramics do not universally require metal powder processing. Industrial catalyst research does not universally wait for ammonia synthesis.
- Concrete has several binder routes; Portland cement is not a compulsory predecessor for all concrete construction.
- Organized fire response can precede a modern emergency center. Useful exits are relevant outside planned urban street networks.
- Steel frames can use qualified riveted or threaded connections. Additional welding routes still require explicit supporting discoveries.
- Lightweight aircraft need not originate in timber. Retractable gear can use manual or fluid-powered mechanisms before electronics.
- Aircraft maintenance records do not require a construction-industry record system. Hydrogen aircraft can explore distinct qualified conversion routes rather than assuming one engine type.

These are proposed causal relationships informed by mechanism and selected sources. Full historical review, semantic reconciliation and physical production closure remain open.
