## Distinctions and branch review

Cargo lashing restrains discrete loads; grain restraints address shifting loose cargo; moisture qualification checks a different bulk hazard; loading sequences control hull loading during transfer. They are not four names for higher cargo capacity. Multibeam surveys obtain depth swaths, side scan images reflectivity and candidate features, and motion compensation corrects platform-induced distortion. No chart grants omniscience.

Shore electricity serves a vessel's electrical loads and does not require electric propulsion. Vessel broadcasts report identities without guaranteeing authenticity, so cryptographic identity is not a parent. Traffic separation assigns lanes, while shore traffic coordination supplies a staffed movement service. Dynamic stationkeeping can use a qualified local reference instead of satellites. It remains constrained by available thrust and power.

Existing inclining evidence within ship stability, sound-speed assumptions within echo sounding, generic damage control, refrigerated freight, ballast trimming and hull condition surveys were reviewed rather than added again. Hull inspection access is specifically physical access construction, not another inspection procedure. The specialized thermal and gas records remain proposed qualified systems; this document supplies no real operating parameters or regulatory certification.

All twenty have per-record operating requirements, a local demonstration, and five disadvantaged recovery contracts: paid scholar travel, reciprocal trials, commissioned research, equipment or service purchase, and sample analysis. Each route retains local work, delay, finite supplier capacity and resource requirements. These authored contracts still need runtime consumers and behavioral verification.
