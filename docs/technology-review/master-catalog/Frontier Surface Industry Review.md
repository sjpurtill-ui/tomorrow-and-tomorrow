# Frontier surface industry review

Eighteen new authored capabilities extend the existing space catalog into sustained surface industry. These are conditional future design proposals, not claims of current deployment. No new IDs duplicate the existing propellant depot, oxygen extraction, dust exclusion or landing-pad integration records.

| Proposed capability | Distinct operating consequence |
|---|---|
| Surface Illumination Horizon Mapping | Predicts usable sunlight windows for particular sites; distant ridges and seasonal geometry can invalidate apparent continuous supply. |
| Shadowed Surface Route Planning | Opens bounded access to shadowed deposits; poor reserves force a shorter sortie instead of a guaranteed resource haul. |
| Surface Survival Heat Budgeting | Trades production against survival duration; loss of reserve can damage equipment even while settlement stock remains elsewhere. |
| Surface Cold-Start Recommissioning | Restores verified capacity gradually after darkness; a successful survival cycle does not instantly yield full plant output. |
| Surface Power Cable Deployment | Connects distant loads within cable capacity and losses; cuts and failed connectors interrupt the affected branch rather than all sites gaining global power. |
| Vacuum Dust Connector Servicing | Returns a qualified connector to service; worn interfaces consume spares and can remain unusable after cleaning. |
| Regolith Tool Wear Management | Schedules replacement before loss of cutting performance; local mining creates a continuing imported-or-manufactured tool demand. |
| Low-Gravity Excavation Reaction Control | Raises achievable excavation throughput for supported ground; excessive force causes slip or immobilization instead of free Earthlike digging. |
| Regolith Feedstock Classification | Diverts unsuitable fractions before reactor or construction work; classification redistributes feed and cannot create its missing constituents. |
| Regolith Volatile Capture | Produces a measured contaminated condensate inventory with extraction losses; a survey indication alone supplies nothing. |
| Regolith Condensate Purification | Makes an accepted water fraction available for further processing; unqualified fractions cannot directly become drinking water or electrolyzer feed. |
| Regolith Sintered Pavers | Supplies finite paving units from local material while paying energy and reject costs; manufactured units alone do not certify a launch pad. |
| Vacuum Construction Joint Qualification | Permits a specified interface for supported loads; tested mechanical or welded options retain separate equipment and repair dependencies. |
| Surface Cargo Offloading | Turns landed cargo into locally reachable stock through finite handling work; items too heavy or inaccessible remain at the lander. |
| Regolith Shield Placement | Changes protection only for the treated occupied volume; excess load or blocked access can disable the structure. |
| Surface Spares Cannibalization | Returns one asset to service at the expense of donor capability; no duplicated component inventory or automatic equivalence between models. |
| Surface Process Heat Cascading | Reduces a specified secondary heat demand while the source runs; source shutdown removes the recovered supply and forces reserves or load reduction. |
| Surface Dust Witness Monitoring | Triggers evidence-based inspection or cleaning of affected equipment; a clean witness does not certify hidden seals or every nearby surface. |

## Causal structure and player choices

Illumination mapping supports shadow-route planning and survival budgeting. Survival requires either real stored energy or a qualified fission supply; technology names alone do not generate energy. Recommissioning then restores verified loads gradually. Physical cabling extends distribution only to connected loads.

Excavation remains the existing basic capability. New force control concerns limited reaction forces; wear management concerns continuing tool replacement. Feed classification supports sintered units, while volatile capture and purification produce distinct intermediate inventories. Direct deposit access is an alternative to moving all feed into an excavator. Imported consumables remain possible; successful local extraction never eliminates unrelated spare-part dependencies.

The empirical flour-sifting alternative contributes a screening principle to feed classification. It does not qualify a food sieve for lunar duty. Equipment, energy and low-gravity process validation remain explicit operating requirements. Construction joints allow mechanical-seal or welding foundations; each retains its material and tooling obligations.

Every record has a disadvantaged acquisition contract. Paid physical envoys need travel and life support; remote guidance has delay and local-skill limits. Partnerships consume real experimental contributions. Purchased research delivers bounded evidence with uncertain success. Imported modules can operate only when installed and supplied, and do not grant local manufacturing mastery. Cannibalization removes donor capability instead of duplicating inventory.

## Evidence and uncertainty

These sources establish the engineering concerns. Prerequisite edges, game inventory boundaries and numerical tuning are design work and are not certified by the sources. No universal performance figures are assigned.

- [NASA’s lunar South Pole environment overview](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/moonbase-environment/) identifies terrain-dependent illumination, prolonged cold and dust effects on equipment. This motivates site-specific survival and servicing requirements.
- [NASA surface power technology](https://www.nasa.gov/power/) includes physical cable deployment and coordinated power delivery. Distributed loads therefore require actual connections.
- [NASA’s lunar-water technology assessment](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230010039) treats extraction, capture, purification and electrolysis as an integrated process under development. Capture and purification here remain separate from existing electrolysis and oxygen-production knowledge.
- [NASA’s ISRU overview](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-in-situ-resource-utilization/) discusses coupled resource-processing systems. Game output remains limited by observed feed and process inputs.
- [NASA’s landing-pad concepts study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20205008550/downloads/Landing%20Launch%20Pads%20Concepts%20v3%20Poster.pdf) includes feed preparation and construction alternatives. Sintered paving units are proposed supply inputs; existing launch-pad stabilization retains system-level acceptance.
- [NASA’s dust research overview](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/nasa-technologies-aim-to-solve-housekeepings-biggest-issue-dust/) describes continuing testing of dust behavior and mitigation. Inspection and local qualification are retained rather than granting dust immunity.
