# Aviation subsystem coverage

Twenty-four authored proposals extend D11 from67 to91 of160 planned identities. They remain unimplemented proposals. Existing broad aircraft-control, flight-pressure, icing, rotor and landing-gear topics do not supply these components automatically. Each additional identity names a distinct physical mechanism, a specific installation or observation and an actual proposed consumer.

Wingtip geometry alters induced drag; natural laminar surfaces and powered suction address boundary-layer transition with different hardware and maintenance. Variable sweep uses loaded pivots, spoiler deployment removes lift, servo tabs exploit aerodynamic moments, and yaw damping requires rate sensing and bounded feedback. These are selected mechanisms beneath broad flight-control integration, not alternative names for learning to fly.

Crossfeed routes a real tank to an engine and is not tank-to-tank fuel transfer. Inerting must continually supply qualified oxygen-depleted air. Capacitive gauging produces uncertain measurements rather than exact hidden inventory. A ram-air turbine depends on airflow; an auxiliary turbine burns fuel; electrical bus isolation can preserve finite priority supply only by dropping other demand. None produces free energy.

Fire loops provide observations and do not extinguish a fire. Thrust reversal requires actual flow, deployed hardware and locks. An overrunning rotor coupling provides no powered lift; rotor damping and track corrections are distinct from aggregate flight control or fatigue bookkeeping. Strut energy absorption, shimmy damping and a one-use thermal vent have different states and consequences. Air-cycle refrigeration uses heat rejection and expansion work; pneumatic boots deform to shed demonstrated ice rather than heating every exposed surface.

Every proposal requires compatible capital, consumable inputs, finite work or service, operating limits, partial/saved state and the named downstream consumer. Imported equipment may support a known operation without teaching its manufacture. Five external-learning contracts require attributable information, paid local trials or finite external service. Runtime promotion also requires tests for faults, payment, repeat-day behavior and actor isolation where applicable. There is no global safety, lift, drag or reliability multiplier.
