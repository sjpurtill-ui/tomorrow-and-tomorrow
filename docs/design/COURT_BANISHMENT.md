# Court banishment

Successful exile and expulsion judgments now receive a distinct court performance.
Two eligible adult supporters approach the adjudicated person. After a short
confrontation, the first supporter indicates the room's actual exit; the target
turns and walks out between the supporters with free hands. The checked escort
paths, physical spacing, separate guard returns and camera ownership reuse the
validated custody implementation. No restraint or injury is added by this scene.

The engine remains the authority. Typed orders, foreign-envoy menu judgments,
known-person exile and the official Cast out action must all supply successful
results with the exact target identity. Refusal, hesitation, failed or no-op
results and absent targets must retain their factual fallback. Exile's existing
population and office changes occur once in the engine; the animation changes
neither. Prior visible bonds are removed when a detained person is exiled.

The default detention performance is unchanged. Skip, close, reduced motion and
stalled movement use the existing cleanup and final-outcome behavior. Existing
age eligibility applies to all participants. No save fields or migration change.
