# Who are we becoming?

This replaces the old founding-focus screen for interactive new games. Four ambitions are visible with their research tradeoffs before selection. Choosing one completes the founding time gate, updates the banner and starts the map at normal speed. No old survival preset is applied. Existing worlds and explicit legacy capture presets remain compatible.

F8 reopens the direction screen. One ambition directs research toward two fields (+25%) at a smaller cost to other research (-5%). Its associated official cultural value moves gradually toward 80/100 at at most 2.92 points per year. Ambitions can be changed after one year; they do not grant discoveries or accelerate the calendar.

Three authored, non-expiring visions become available: the first after thirty game days, later ones at least 180 days after the previous answer. Named historical figures provide perspectives; absent figures are represented as community voices. Each answer changes one official value by four points. There are no compulsory crisis popups, expiring choices, automatic migration or fabricated diplomatic encounters. Values feed the existing society/institution model. The most recent 24 directional decisions are retained.

Routine work remains owned by the current GovernmentPeopleSystem and its daily settlement delegation, including food and water safeguards. The older ambition-specific allocator is retired; its saved fields and no-op method remain compatible. The ambition screen does not override civic labor instructions.

State is included in the military export/import payload, with validation and rollback. Tests cover research effects, slow cultural change, duplicate/day guards, decision timing and non-expiry, labor conservation, no fabricated supplies, manual override, JSON persistence, and the real opening-to-map interaction.

This is the first culture-and-ambition loop. Autonomous expeditions, negotiations, additional eras of authored visions and broader relationship stories are follow-up work.
