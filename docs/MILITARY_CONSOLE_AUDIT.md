## Prioritized findings

1. **Confirmed stale display while reading.** command_rail_hud.gd:711–719 suppresses every background dock rebuild while the mouse is anywhere over that dock, or any input has nonempty text. It protects buttons/tooltips from destruction but also freezes live training labels. An isolated probe advanced authoritative training while the pointer was inside the probe panel: the render count did not change. Moving the panel away from the isolated pointer allowed immediate refresh. The player's game was not advanced or altered. Fix recommendation: update stable progress/status labels in place; defer only structural row changes while typing or clicking. Do not remove interaction protection indiscriminately.

2. **Two different meanings of “training.”** Recruit & Deploy shows progress through a training order. Forces shows the completed formation's proficiency, which can be39% even after its order reaches100%. Poor access to training equipment lowers graduation proficiency. Required training work is seven effective days for basic levies, not seven calendar days. The queue truncates progress to integers while the percentage uses full precision, so the displayed fraction can stay unchanged as the percentage advances. Replace with “Basic training78% complete · aboutN game days remaining at the current rate”; detail should show5.46/7.00 training work, rate, equipment access and class capacity. Save start/completion days for new orders if calendar attendance is to be shown; older records must say unknown. A game-speed setting changes calendar advance per wall-clock second; neither is training work.

3. **Confirmed slower progress, not a proven simulation stall.** The probe's same two one-person levy orders progressed0.34595 effective days per calendar day without practice equipment, versus0.629 with equipment; four game days showed0,0,1,1 after integer truncation while true progress was0.34595,0.69190,1.03785,1.38380. At those constant rates a seven-work-day course takes about21 or12 calendar days. Four times class capacity reduces the base rate to0.15725 before equipment effects. The exact live campaign's training rate was not exposed; these are controlled reproductions, not claims about its private state. Basic recruitment training has a positive equipment-access floor and does not hard-pause solely for missing equipment or food; food/health affect condition and injuries. Separate exercises on the Training tab can explicitly pause for ration or delivery shortages and already hold a paused_reason. Present that reason and distinguish slow from stopped.

4. **“BROKEN” does not mean broken equipment or morale.** MilitaryUnitCatalog derives this word solely from personnel_condition below30%; DEGRADED is below55%. The isolated probe produced BROKEN with full morale. Replace with “Unfit for duty · condition20%” and separate “Equipment1 of3 issued,” “Skill39%,” and morale only if actually represented. The live campaign's earlier BROKEN row was reported verbally; its exact condition/cause was not inspected. Do not diagnose its cause from the word alone.

5. **Repeated rows hide the personnel lifecycle.** Every completed new training order creates a separate aggregate formation, including repeated one-person orders of the same type. A1/1 row means current personnel versus authorized strength, not one army out of one. These are trained soldiers at home until deployed; they are not duplicate civilian people. The template is a reusable composition drawing from a shared home pool, not its own reserved copy of soldiers. Group matching rows in presentation, with expandable batch details where conditions differ. Lead with “2 soldiers ready at home ·2 trainees ·0 deployed.” Keep the user-understood “Garrison3 of8” concept, with an explanatory “assigned / required.”

6. **Stores is ambiguous.** In the defense summary its percentage is the share of the food reserve protected from raids, not the quantity of weapons or all resources. Use “Food protected from raids: N%.” Where equipment is returned, say “returned to equipment reserve.” Keep Recruit & Train and New Build, but label the three steps: choose desired soldiers → recruit/train missing places → deploy trained soldiers. Show exactly how many are already ready, in training, unequipped, and still missing. A deployment button must explain its current blocker.
