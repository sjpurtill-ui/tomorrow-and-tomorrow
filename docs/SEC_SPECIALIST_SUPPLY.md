# Finite PEG laboratory supplies

This is a declared game boundary: a preexisting specialist laboratory archive
becomes available in an established external scientific settlement. It is not a
simulation of local packing synthesis or the manufacture of narrow standards.
It does not establish a full historical bootstrap from raw terrain.

The existing daily society-exchange owner registers the archive once when an
external settled actor has adopted the five size-exclusion foundations, public
schools and nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy, with at least five effective
Knowledge workers and one Logistics worker. The player cannot receive this grant.
It adds 20 units of qualified aqueous SEC packing and 24 each of assigned narrow
PEG DP10, DP40 and DP160 references to that actor's ordinary finite stores. A saved
origin, actor identity and endowment day prevent repeating the endowment after
requests, depletion or reload. Missing optional archive data in older saves means
no prior archive endowment was recorded; no legacy stock is claimed as synthesized.

Local material-accounting and standard-measure knowledge permit the existing
embassy to request at most one missing unit of each material. SEC foundations
must be ready. An actual located contact, payment and travel provisions are
required. The supplier deducts cargo at arrival; the buyer receives it only on
return. Insufficient supplier stock refuses the consignment and returns unused
payment through the existing owner. Finished SEC research can request finite
replacement materials, unlike one-time ore-investigation consignments. No
request generates inventory, refunds used reagents or grants local synthesis.

Qualified material names denote this archive's assigned material class, not an
assay of generic PEG. Preparing solutions, packing/conditioning a column, paying
instrument operation and verifying an observed calibration remain separate work.
The separation instrument, distribution evidence and production consumer are
connected in the isolated branch; see SEC_DISTRIBUTION_MODEL.md. Canonical
integration and final review are still required before implemented counting.

Owned integration files are `sec_specialist_supply.gd`, `society_exchange.gd`,
`research_materials.gd`, `resource_knowledge_catalog.gd`,
`hud/research_purchase_panel.gd` and the additive `polymer_knowledge.gd` entry.
No new economy, travel clock, settlement or save owner is introduced.
