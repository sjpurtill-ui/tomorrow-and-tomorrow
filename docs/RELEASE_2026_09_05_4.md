## Player behavior

Select an army, single-click a city building, then Attack Now or Besiege Now. If farther away, March & Attack / March & Besiege carries the chosen army to the city and starts the operation on arrival. No separate arrival registration is required. Selecting another destination cancels that queued order. Orders persist through save/load. Two armies at one city do not cause the wrong army to be used. A successful hostile contact starts war; departure and failed orders do not. Siege investment and assault remain distinct.

Battle start pauses time and opens the battle graphics at round 0. Automatic threat resolution respects that pause. Aftermath is surfaced when combat ends and when a saved unresolved outcome is loaded; opening the screen does not decide a policy.

At close zoom, opaque counter plates stop covering soldier figures. Ground fitting uses the actual close terrain surface. Forces of up to 16 use one representative per soldier; larger forces remain bounded. Occupation troops appear at the known city with a YOUR GARRISON label and a Manage Garrison action. Zero-active field armies disappear from map markers and active selectors; recovery, battle history, population and occupation records remain intact.

Routine deaths are grouped by place, cause and 30-day period into compact count rows. Original demographic records remain intact. This is deterministic presentation, not generated biographies or an LLM call.
