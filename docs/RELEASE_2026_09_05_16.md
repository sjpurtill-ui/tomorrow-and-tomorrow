# Release 2026.09.05.16 — nearby societies and a continuing history

New worlds generate up to three nearby societies on connected land while keeping distant rivals. They receive no free contact or revealed homeland. Existing worlds retain their recorded geography. Small islands may legitimately have no connected regional society.

After a returned encounter, the known-record screen offers an objective: ask scouts to find that society's settlement. They organize the shortest viable expedition using real route, capacity, personnel and food checks; duplicate dispatch is blocked. Findings become available after return. Detailed expedition controls remain optional.

Temporary dominance or twelve turns of distress no longer declare the whole civilization journey over. A bounded chronicle records observed developments, recoveries and influence, with a legacy account available from 2,500 years. Local war outcomes and occupation rules remain. This is lifecycle groundwork; quiet-time pacing and durable leadership/cultural interaction remain pending. The earlier year-20 gate was earliest qualification, not proof of an observed natural win at year 20.
