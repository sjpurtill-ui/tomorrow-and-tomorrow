# 2026.09.10.4 — scouts leave on standing orders

Recruitment and influence no longer requires spare homes before any party may depart. Staff can visit communities to build goodwill and exchange knowledge while household invitations wait for housing, food, water and reception support. If no usable community is known, parties search through physical travel; they do not create newcomers.

Staff can organize smaller affordable parties, consider longer expeditions, and move past an unreachable destination. The seven-day home food reserve, adult commitments, land routes and finite migration rules remain in force. Exploration can reach a more distant frontier after nearby ground is charted.

Open **Scouting** for separate departure status and invitation readiness, specific shortages, and the next automatic review. Existing policies and journeys remain compatible; older saves gain a default destination-rotation position.
