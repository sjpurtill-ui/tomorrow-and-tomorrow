# Landscape iteration 6 — seasons from the existing climate

Task worktree `/Users/seanpurtill/Documents/Codex/tt-seasonal-cover`, branch `codex/seasonal-cover`, based on canonical Mac `d07dc6e01b3404ccc70ff40876bdd605ee69c39c`. Sole integrator owns this delivery. Player PID 74445 remains on the earlier packaged release; it was not interrupted.

Ground cover, close crowns, scrub, forest floors and broad vegetation now respond to the simulation calendar and local seasonal temperature. Temperate cover moves toward dry stems/litter in its cold season. The two hemispheres reverse together with food production. Bare drylands remain bare, and warm tropical vegetation stays green when its climate never becomes cold. Seasonal changes update bounded material uniforms without rebuilding geography, moving trees, changing their count, or altering resources. Existing atlas imagery, terrain precision, fog, geometry and camera distances remain in use.

The authority audit found that food already uses PlanetEnvironment's mean temperature, latitude/continentality amplitude and hemisphere-aware annual wave. Its weather-yield factor is an aggregate production modifier, not a physical rainfall or snow record. The HUD had a separate temperature amplitude, a north-only wave, and an independent daily wobble. It now uses the same ambient-temperature function as food, projected from the actual local climate. Food's arithmetic and environmental profiles are unchanged; the function extraction is shared by all civilizations. No new weather or resource model was added.

Terrain carries the existing seasonal amplitude as one additional float per vertex in CUSTOM0, leaving climate UVs, geological UV2, woodland alpha, heights, normals and indices intact. Both sliced builders, the synchronous close fallback and the base mesh supply it. Crowns carry climate/amplitude once in MultiMesh custom data; forest-floor/legacy materials use their local sample. Updates follow GameState.elapsed_days at a 0.1-day threshold, including loading/rewinding, and stop when time is paused. Weak references drop expired materials. The 600,000-vertex terrain cache limit is unchanged; the extra amplitude channel accounts for up to 2.4 MB of raw cached vertex data. Godot's full-float custom channel format is documented at https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_mesh.html.
