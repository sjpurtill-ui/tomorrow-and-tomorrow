## Behavior

Seven distinct discoveries: `threshing_frames`, `winnowing_practice`, `grain_moisture_testing`, `forced_air_grain_drying`, `roller_grain_milling`, `flour_sifting`, and `grain_malting`. The existing `grain_milling` also operates paid hand-mill equipment. No discovery grants equipment or free food.

The module maintains a per-settlement grain/fraction ledger in ration-equivalent food energy. Threshing reclassifies at most a quarter of current cultivation as the model's aggregate cereal share; old mixed staples, dried wild plants, and incoming trade are not relabeled as grain. Winnowing and sampling lose small amounts. Assessed batches can be dried with electricity; manual or powered milling makes flour. Sifting returns both fine flour and edible bran. Malting reserves only surplus beyond a week's demand, prepays water/fuel, requires four days and available finishing handlers, and returns less food energy. Germinating food is unavailable for issue and remains subject to spoilage and storage limits.

Installed equipment costs actual available raw materials or manufactured equipment. The same local steward rule for player/rival cities commissions one affordable setup against available inputs while a week's food remains; the home research inspector also offers explicit installation. Handling shares at most 20% of the Logistics time remaining after cooking, and preservation receives the remainder. The primary settlement's powered demand feeds the ordinary finite generation/service ledger. Secondary cities currently use manual methods because existing TechnologyOperations power is primary-only.

Prepared grain fractions are consumed as staples; raw grain remains available when ordinary staples run out. The small preparation-quality contribution applies only to processed rations actually eaten. Issue, external trade and founding-convoy withdrawals debit the same finite stock once. Food net flow now reports the actual change in available stocks, including processing transfers/losses. The food report shows grain fractions, unavailable germinating food, handling effort and losses.
