The terrain material previously blended a satellite photograph containing unrelated mountains into the generated planet. Those photographed ridges were not the physical landforms used for movement or surveys. The terrain no longer loads that image. Neutral ground/forest texture supplies surface grain; actual terrain geometry and normals supply relief. Coarse regional normals average two cells and extra hillshade contrast is gentler, reducing alternating dark/light facets without changing vertex heights or indices.

All three real terrain mesh paths carry precipitation, warmth and normalized sedimentary/igneous/metamorphic family weights. Climate comes from LocalTerrain's actual climate field; geology comes from the same PlanetEnvironment function used by resource profiles. The existing vertex-color woodland channel remains intact. Missing fields on custom/legacy meshes retain a fallback. The optional builder arguments preserve older callers.

Hot dry ground now blends exposed earth with the local geological family; cold dry ground shifts toward muted mineral soil. Wet ground darkens subtly. Steep faces expose geological rock even at low elevations, instead of an altitude multiplier disguising coastal cliffs as grassy slopes. Exposed sedimentary, igneous and metamorphic families vary in tone and grain. Fine detail filters by projected pixel size. Integer cell hashing prevents floating multiply/fract precision loss in distant parts of the 40,075 km world.
