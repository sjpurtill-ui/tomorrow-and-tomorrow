# Culture: years 1200–1800

**Scope.** This file is the **Culture** line for game years 1200–1800 (`culture` dynamic and direction). It has four channels: Social cohesion, Shared legitimacy, Inquiry breadth and Collective memory, and it continues `y600/CULTURE_600_1200.md`. It covers relics and pilgrimage, communities of devotees under a rule, the great houses of the god (their building is shared with Infrastructure), images and the dispute over them, chant and music notation, epic, lyric, romance and the novel, histories and chronicles, drama, festivals, games, tournaments, heraldry, knightly codes and painting. The people revere their living god, so relics, rites, pilgrimages and preaching orders are in the god's name, and the god's servants are honoured as holy. No main-catalog culture id has a `HISTORICAL_YEAR` in this window. Schools, universities and book media are in Knowledge. Offices, courts and law are in Institutions. Rows marked [gov: …] change the court or the seat of rule (see the tag legend in the Institutions file).

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` on `origin/codex/research-600` (`[[800,-500],[1500,1000],[2000,1600]]`) maps game 1200 to ≈ AD 360, 1300 to ≈ 570, 1400 to ≈ 790, 1500 to AD 1000, 1600 to ≈ 1120, 1700 to ≈ 1240 and 1800 to ≈ 1360. Years 1200–1500 run at ≈ 2.14 historical years per game year: late antiquity and the successor kingdoms (1200–1400), then the revival of the ninth and tenth centuries (1400–1500). Years 1500–1800 run at 1.2 historical years per game year: the high-medieval growth of towns, schools and royal justice, ending just before gunpowder weapons become decisive. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states and religions are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Culture team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE` unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), in `docs/research/y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200, merge aliases included) or in `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names the 0–600 or 600–1200 item that a row improves, an earlier row of this file, or a row in another 1200–1800 line.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.

The total is **80** advances: 36 in years 1200–1500 and 44 in years 1500–1800. Across the four channels, one lands about every 8 years, so each channel always has work of its age. The second half is denser because each game year there covers 1.2 historical years instead of 2.14. Most items take 4–12 years. Long thresholds take 12–20 years (1.2–2 real hours).

**Key thresholds:**
1. **The god's house:** tomb pilgrimage and relic shrines (1210–1215) → devotees under a rule (1250) → domed great house (1283) → the dispute over images (1400) → terraced temple mountain (1410) → round-arched great houses (1540) → pointed-arch houses of coloured glass (1625) → wandering preacher orders (1675) → jubilee year (1750).
2. **Music:** chant schools (1313) → neume notation (1420) → many voices (1462) → staff lines (1521) → choirbooks (1655) → measured rhythm (1717).
3. **Story and memory:** world history as the god's plan (1230) → peoples' origin histories (1300) → warband poems (1360) → frame stories (1450) → court novel (1507) → book of kings (1508) → champion songs (1583) → courtly love (1590) → romance (1650) → sagas (1690) → allegory epic in the everyday tongue (1758) → town chronicles (1770) → townsfolk tales (1794).
4. **Image:** icons (1275) → interlace books (1370) → landscape scrolls (1530) → ink wash (1640) → lifelike wall painting (1755) → dance of death (1796).
5. **Court and seat of rule (for the civic-evolution pass):** `official_ivory_diptychs` (1265, court: gifts of new officials), `race_faction_parties` (1285, court: street factions sway the ruler), `court_learning_revival` (1405, court: scholars and poets join it), `mirror_for_rulers` (1422, court), `coronation_regalia_rite` (1466, seat: crown, orb and staff in the throne room), `knightly_conduct_code` (1612, court), `heraldic_arms` (1620, court: arms hang in the hall and on seals), `wandering_preacher_orders` (1675, court: preachers become confessors and advisers), `ruler_town_entries` (1745, seat: the ruler's entry into towns), `chivalric_orders_insignia` (1785, court: an order of knights around the ruler).
6. **Ownership of overlaps:** the buildings of the great houses are shared with Infrastructure. Armed pilgrimage is shared with Security. Tournaments are Security's (`tournament_training`, 1647). Pilgrim roads are shared with Logistics. Work bells and hours are Labor's (`communal_bell_hours` 1425, `work_bell_hours` 1755). The pointed arch and rib vault as techniques are Infrastructure (1410, 1582). The dance of death is shared with Health. Staff notation stays here, not in Knowledge. This list was checked against the other eleven 1200–1800 lists, and its duplicates were removed.
