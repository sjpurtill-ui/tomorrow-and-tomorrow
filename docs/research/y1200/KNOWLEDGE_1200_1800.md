# Knowledge: years 1200–1800

**Scope.** This file is the **Knowledge** line for game years 1200–1800 (`knowledge` dynamic; `direction: "Information"`). It has four channels: Observers, Directed attention, Preserved knowledge and Communication, and it continues `y600/KNOWLEDGE_600_1200.md`. It covers book hands and page layout, paper and printing, copying houses, schools and universities, number and algebra, sky tables and instruments, clocks, optics and experiment, the science of motion, maps and signals. Catalog ids placed here: `relief_block_cutting`, `printing_process`, `chemical_distillation`, `movable_type_composition`, `optical_lenses` and `wooden_movable_type`. All were marked `belongs_later` in `registry_1200.json`. `paper_sheet_pressing` is placed by Production (1422). Items owned by other lines are left out: the magnetic compass, rudders and sea charts (Logistics); paper mills, water and wind power (Production); hospitals and medicine (Health); law schools, examinations and chancery clerks (Institutions); music notation, chronicles and literature (Culture). Rows marked [gov: …] change government or civic life. The civic-evolution pass should read them together with the Institutions list.

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` on `origin/codex/research-600` (`[[800,-500],[1500,1000],[2000,1600]]`) maps game 1200 to ≈ AD 360, 1300 to ≈ 570, 1400 to ≈ 790, 1500 to AD 1000, 1600 to ≈ 1120, 1700 to ≈ 1240 and 1800 to ≈ 1360. Years 1200–1500 run at ≈ 2.14 historical years per game year: late antiquity and the successor kingdoms (1200–1400), then the revival of the ninth and tenth centuries (1400–1500). Years 1500–1800 run at 1.2 historical years per game year: the high-medieval growth of towns, schools and royal justice, ending just before gunpowder weapons become decisive. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states and religions are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Knowledge team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE` unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), in `docs/research/y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200, merge aliases included) or in `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names the 0–600 or 600–1200 item that a row improves, an earlier row of this file, or a row in another 1200–1800 line.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.

The total is **84** advances: 39 in years 1200–1500 and 45 in years 1500–1800. Across the four channels, one lands about every 7 years, so each channel always has work of its age. The second half is denser because each game year there covers 1.2 historical years instead of 2.14. Most items take 4–12 years. Long thresholds take 12–20 years (1.2–2 real hours).

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Script and the page:** rounded book hand (1206) → hard-word glossaries (1252) → word spacing (1362) → small-letter book hand (1397) → concordances and indexes (1692) → numbered leaves and running heads (1742) → commonplace books (1784).
2. **Printing and paper:** relief blocks (1355) → `printing_process` (1360) → pressed paper sheets (Production `paper_sheet_pressing`, 1422) → block-printed books (1441) → printed classics (1465) → clay movable type (1533) → paper working copies (1612) → wooden type (1746). Press printing with cast metal type belongs later (≈ 1867).
3. **Schools:** seven-arts manuals (1227) → retreat copying rooms (1286) → royal house of learning (1422) → great-house schools (1494) → chartered university (1625) → translation school (1630) → scholastic method (1650) → endowed colleges (1700) → reckoning schools (1735).
4. **Number:** nine digits and zero (1267) → rules for zero and debts (1326) → restoring-and-balancing algebra (1419) → decimal fractions (1478) → algebra of powers (1500) → coefficient triangle (1546) → cubics by conics (1558) → merchants' digit reckoning (1668) → root extraction (1707). Symbolic algebra belongs later.
5. **Sky, time and trial:** astrolabe (1340) → escapement sky globe (1372) → great-instrument observatory (1425) → darkened-room optics (1518) → clock tower (1573) → program of trials (1718) → lenses (1733) → verge clock (1737) → striking equal hours (1775) → mean speed, impetus and graphs (1778–1792).
6. **Government and civic life (for the civic-evolution pass):** `royal_house_of_learning` (1422) puts librarians, translators and sky-watchers into the court. `letter_writing_art` (1567) trains the clerks who staff Institutions' `royal_chancery_office` (1560). `chartered_university` (1625) creates a sworn corporation with its own privileges and courts beside the town. Its graduates become court councillors and jurists (Institutions `glossator_law_schools`, 1602). `printed_standard_classics` (1465) fixes the texts for Institutions' examinations. `striking_equal_hour_clock` (1775) puts a clock on the town hall or tower and sets civic hours for markets and work (shared: Labor). `timed_beacon_code` (1432) links the court to its frontiers.
7. **Ownership of overlaps:** law faculties are Institutions. Music notation, chronicles and vernacular literature are Culture. Paper mills are Production. The compass and sea charts are Logistics. Paper sheets and paper mills are Production (`paper_sheet_pressing` 1422, `paper_stamping_mills` 1676). Mineral acids are shared with Production. Herb waters and spirits that continue `chemical_distillation` are Health (1505) and Nutrition (1722). The needle compass is Logistics (`floating_needle_compass`, 1600), which `magnetic_declination_note` continues. The realm beacon chain is Security (`realm_beacon_chain`, 1420), which `timed_beacon_code` continues. Daily weather diaries are Ecology (1795). Reading spectacles are Health (1740). Guild schools for apprentices are Labor (1750); `reckoning_schools` covers merchants' sons.
