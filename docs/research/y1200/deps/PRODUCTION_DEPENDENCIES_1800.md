# Production dependencies, years 1200–1800

Machine-readable source: `partials/pils.json`. The format follows `docs/research/deps/MAPPING_CONTRACT.md` and the 600–1200 partials. Cross-line ids are marked with their line. Earlier-block ids are marked "0–600" or "600–1200". `Requires (any)` groups are separated by `;`. Every prerequisite and precedent is dated strictly before its dependent. No year moves are proposed (`partials/pils_year_adjustments.json` is empty).

The iron chain runs `liquid_iron_furnaces` (600–1200) and `crucible_steel_cakes` → `co_fusion_steel` (1345), and `cast_iron_vessels` → `monumental_iron_casting` (1352). `bloomery_smelting` → `coal_fired_ironworking` (1525, needs Coal). `water_driven_bellows` (600–1200) + `bloomery_charge_control` → `water_blown_stack_bloomery` (1706) → **`blast_furnace`** (1772). The blast furnace also requires `water_driven_bellows` and `liquid_iron_furnaces`. Then `blast_furnace` + `water_powered_hammers` → **`finery_forges`** (1794). `standard_bar_iron` (1645) requires `water_powered_hammers`.

The power chain is `water_mills` (893) → floating and horizontal-wheel mills (1262–1275) → `tide_mills` (1332, needs `tide_reckoning`) → `cog_and_lantern_gearing` (1370) → `cam_motion_design` (1545, also needs `water_powered_hammers`) → `fulling_mills` (1558), `water_ore_stamps` (1582) → `ore_washing_sluices`, and on to `post_windmills` (1654, gearing + post-and-beam joinery). Vertical-axis wind towers (1462) come from the flat water wheel, not from gearing. The hand crank of `hand_crank_winnower` (nutrition, 600–1200) → `crank_grindstones` (1420) → `water_grinding_wheels`, `crank_driven_sawmills` (1698, with `water_powered_stone_saws`).

Paper: `paper_making` (1090) + `mechanical_screw_presses` → `paper_sheet_pressing` (1422) → **`paper_stamping_mills`** (1676, also `water_mills` and `cam_motion_design`) → `gelatin_paper_sizing` (1702) and `paper_watermarks` (1744, with `wire_drawing`).

Ceramics: `high_fire_stoneware` → `pale_hard_fired_ware` (1248) → **`kaolin_porcelain`** (1492) → `underglaze_cobalt_blue` (1782). `lead_glazed_ware` + `tin_smelting` → **`tin_opacified_glaze`** (1405, needs Tin Ore) → `lustre_glazed_ware` (1415).

Glass: `glass_blowing` → `trailed_glass_vessels` (1315). `decolorized_clear_glass` → `potash_forest_glass` (1435). `cast_window_glass` + `lead_sheet_rolling` → **`leaded_stained_glass`** (1608) → `silver_stain_glass` (1756). `blown_cylinder_panes` → `spun_crown_panes` (1736).

Textiles: `drop_spindles` → **`sericulture_reeling`** (1288) → silk tapestry, lampas silks, velvet and **`silk_throwing_mills`** (1748, gearing + water mill). `pole_lathe_turning` → `roller_cotton_gin` (1300) → `bow_fibre_fluffing` (1310) → `fustian_cloth` (1636). **`spinning_wheels`** (1498) requires `drop_spindles` and either reeled silk or bowed cotton, since the wheel came from silk quilling and cotton spinning.

Precious metals: `argentiferous_lead_working` → `salt_cementation_parting` (1222) → `acid_gold_parting` (1766, needs knowledge `mineral_acid_distillation`, 1752). `die_struck_coinage` + `touchstone_testing` → `assay_hallmarks` (1752).
