# Logistics dependencies, years 1200–1800

Machine-readable source: `partials/pils.json`. The format follows `docs/research/deps/MAPPING_CONTRACT.md` and the 600–1200 partials. Cross-line ids are marked with their line. Earlier-block ids are marked "0–600" or "600–1200". `Requires (any)` groups are separated by `;`. Every prerequisite and precedent is dated strictly before its dependent. No year moves are proposed (`partials/pils_year_adjustments.json` is empty).

Draft: `breast_strap_harness` (600–1200) → **`rigid_horse_collar`** (1300) → `whippletree_evener` (1415) → `tandem_file_harness` (1515) → `horse_freight_wagons` (1640). `iron_hoof_boots` + `forged_iron_nails` → **`nailed_horseshoes`** (1455). Nutrition's `horse_ploughing` (1560) is expected to require `rigid_horse_collar`.

Hulls: `grown_frame_selection` + `frame_moulding` → `spaced_tenon_planking` (1270) → precedent of `clinker_shell_construction` (1340, caulking + forged nails) → **`keeled_sailing_longships`** (1385) → `open_sea_cargo_ships` (1425). `frame_moulding` + `grown_frame_selection` → **`carvel_frame_construction`** (1540). `paired_quarter_rudders` + `smithing_tool_sets` → **`sternpost_rudder`** (1685) → **`high_sided_bulk_ships`** (1692) → `annual_bulk_fleets`, `cask_tonnage_rating`.

Navigation: `wayfinding_stars` → `land_finding_signs` (1395) → **`floating_needle_compass`** (1600, also `wire_drawing`) → **`compass_bearing_sea_charts`** (1742, with `written_sailing_directions`) and **`dry_compass_card`** (1757). `latitude_zones` (knowledge, 600–1200) → `pole_star_latitude_tablet` (1465).

Water routes: `flash_lock_gates` → `canal_locks` (infrastructure 1493) → `reservoir_fed_summit_canals` (1735, with `trunk_canals`). `towpaths` + `rigid_horse_collar` → `horse_towed_barges` (1622).

Posts and payment: `state_post_passes` → `fee_kept_post_stations` (1240) and, with `post_horse_studs`, **`realm_relay_post`** (1703). `distant_payment_letters` → `remittance_certificates` (1330) and, with `money_changers`, **`bills_of_exchange`** (1650) → `premium_sea_insurance` (1788). `seasonal_trade_gathering` + `market_peacekeepers` → **`guarded_trade_fairs`** (1580) → with `public_weighhouses`, `staple_market_towns` (1762).

**Gap: no magnetic observation precedes the compass.** The brief's pattern is compass ← magnetic observations. No lodestone or magnetism item is dated before 1600. The Knowledge items `magnetic_declination_note` (1612) and `lodestone_pole_experiments` (1724) both come after the floating needle and both presuppose a needle. So `floating_needle_compass` requires `wire_drawing` (the iron needle) and `land_finding_signs` (open-sea wayfinding practice). The two Knowledge items are precedents of the chart and the dry card only. Knowledge should map `magnetic_declination_note` to require `floating_needle_compass`, not the reverse.
