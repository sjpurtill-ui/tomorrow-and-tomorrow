# Infrastructure dependencies, years 1200–1800

Machine-readable source: `partials/pils.json`. The format follows `docs/research/deps/MAPPING_CONTRACT.md` and the 600–1200 partials. Cross-line ids are marked with their line. Earlier-block ids are marked "0–600" or "600–1200". `Requires (any)` groups are separated by `;`. Every prerequisite and precedent is dated strictly before its dependent. No year moves are proposed (`partials/pils_year_adjustments.json` is empty).

The dome chain is `vaulting_tubes` → `thin_brick_shell_vaults` (1270), and `squinch_domes` + `domed_masonry_roofs` → **`pendentive_domes`** (1285) → `buttressing_half_domes`, `cross_domed_halls` (1402), `double_shell_domes` (1572) and, with pointed arches, `intersecting_rib_domes` (1448).

The arch and vault chain is `voussoir_arch_assembly` → `horseshoe_arches` (1360) and **`pointed_arches`** (1410). `pointed_arches` + `cross_vaults` → **`rib_vaults`** (1582) → `clustered_piers` (1628). `dressed_stone_masonry` + `cross_vaults` → `masonry_buttressing` (1550) → with rib vaults, **`flying_buttresses`** (1648), and `buttress_pinnacles` (1655). `pointed_arches` + `glazed_windows` → **`bar_tracery_windows`** (1692) → `rose_windows` (1704). `template_based_sizing` + `straightedge_compass` → `tracing_floor_templates` (1698) → `geometric_design_rules` (1766).

The water chain is `arcaded_aqueduct_bridges` → `long_distance_conduits` (1205) → with `stamped_lead_pipes`, `piped_town_conduits` (1620). `spillway_reservoirs` → `reservoir_valve_pits` (1225) and, with `diversion_dams`, `great_tank_reservoirs` (1240). `flood_levees` → `estuary_embankments` (1498, coast) → `self_closing_tide_gates` (1590).

**`canal_locks`** (1493) is owned here and shared with Logistics. It requires `flash_lock_gates` (logistics, 600–1200), its registry predecessor, and `canal_sluice_gates` (600–1200).

Houses and towns: `upper_storeys` + `kiln_fired_bricks` → **`wall_chimneys`** (1642) → `tiled_stoves` (1754). `timber_roof_trusses` + `dressed_stone_masonry` → `covered_cloth_halls` (1716) → with production `pit_cast_bells`, **`belfry_town_halls`** (1735). `lead_sheet_rolling` → `lead_sheet_roofing` (1255) → `roof_flashing_interfaces` and `lead_rain_gutters`.
