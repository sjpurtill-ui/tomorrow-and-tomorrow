# Health: years 1800–2400

**Scope.** The **Health** research line (`health` dynamic; `direction: "Wellbeing"`). It covers treatment, surgery, drugs and pharmacy, anatomy and physiology, hospitals and nursing, and the licensing of healers. It also covers the answer to pestilence: quarantine, health magistracies, cordons and inoculation. Birth, midwifery, infant care and vital statistics belong to Demography and are marked shared where they touch Health. Water supply, drains and street paving belong to Infrastructure. Continues `docs/research/y1200/HEALTH_1200_1800.md`. Catalog ids placed here: `litter_bearer_drill` (`field_medicine.gd`, HISTORICAL_YEAR AD 1792 ≈ 2384) and `preventive_inoculation` (`society_knowledge_catalog.gd`, catalog AD 1854 ≈ 2544, placed at 2302). `registry_1800.json` marked `preventive_inoculation` `belongs_later` (≈ 2545). Its own description is controlled exposure that reduces later epidemics despite immediate individual risk. That describes variolation, which the brief places late in this window, so it is redated here. Cowpox inoculation (2394) follows it.

**Historical anchor.** `scripts/technology_eras.gd` CURVE `[[1500,1000],[2000,1600],[2400,1800]]` (on `origin/codex/research-600`): game 1800 ≈ AD 1360, 1900 ≈ AD 1480, 2000 ≈ AD 1600, 2100 ≈ AD 1650, 2200 ≈ AD 1700, 2300 ≈ AD 1750, 2400 ≈ AD 1800. Years 1800–2000 run at 1.2 historical years per game year: the aftermath of the great pestilence, gunpowder, the printing press and the first ocean crossings. Years 2000–2400 run at 0.5: the early-modern state, the scientific revolution and the Enlightenment. Each game year there covers only six months of history, so items sit closer together. The window stops before steam-powered factories. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states and religions are calibration only. (regional) items suit a monsoon-river, east-continental or hot dry-land climate or tradition and should be gated by terrain or culture. **(contact)** items need contact with an ocean-crossed continent, or with the lands where the crop, drug or disease comes from, through Logistics' `transoceanic_contact_voyages` (1912). A civilization that has not made that contact never sees them.

**Research time.** Game years of staffed research on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (1 game year ≈ 6 min; 6 y ≈ 37 min; 10 y ≈ 61 min; 15 y ≈ 91 min; 20 y ≈ 2 h). At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today. `NEW · slug` = not authored yet. No slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), `docs/research/y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200), `docs/research/y1200/registry_1800.json` (1200–1800), the game's baked research blocks, `scripts/*.gd` or `technology_eras.gd` `HISTORICAL_YEAR`. "(continues: id)" names the earlier item a row improves: an earlier registry item or an earlier row. `(shared: X)` straddles another line, and `(culture)` feeds this line from Culture. `[gov: …]` marks rows that change government, the court or civic life.

**"Today" column.** Earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" = in the catalog but never completed in a recorded run; `—` = not in main.

## Pacing

The total is **86** advances: 41 in 1800–2100 and 45 in 2100–2400, one about every 7.0 years. Simple remedies and single techniques take 5–6 years. Institutions and treatises take 8–12 years, and the 22 key thresholds take 8–15 years (49–91 min). The line opens with standing defences against pestilence: forty-day quarantine, health passes, island quarantine stations and standing health magistracies. Then come the new pox from across the ocean, printed anatomy and gunshot surgery. After 2000 it moves quickly. The circulation of the blood, fever bark, state medical colleges, a sanitary cordon along a whole frontier, variolation and the controlled scurvy trial arrive in turn. The line ends with cowpox inoculation.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Pestilence:** thirty-day arrival isolation (1200–1800: 1797) → forty-day quarantine (1822) → health passes (1832) → island quarantine station (1845) → standing health magistracy (1906) → ship bills of health (1921) → corpse searchers (1990) → shut-up plague houses (2016) → frontier sanitary cordon (2240).
2. **Smallpox:** pox and measles told apart (1200–1800: 1458) → folk variolation (2002, regional) → variolation trials (2244) → smallpox hospital (2292) → `preventive_inoculation` (2302) → shallow mass inoculation (2334) → cowpox inoculation (2394).
3. **Anatomy and physiology:** printed dissection manual (1898) → dissection atlas (1952) → tiered anatomy theatre (1995) → circulation of the blood (2056) → capillaries (2124) → wax models (2268) → digestion by experiment (2306) → diseases traced to organs (2326).
4. **Surgery:** field surgery handbook (1912) → gentle gunshot dressing (1948) → amputation ligature (1970) → jointed artificial limbs (1974) → arm-flap nose repair (1998) → screw tourniquet (2236) → lateral lithotomy (2254) → cataract extraction (2298) → `litter_bearer_drill` (2384).
5. **Drugs:** guaiac (1936, contact) → herbals printed from life (1942) → city pharmacopoeia (1962) → chemical medicine (1966) → apothecaries' company (2036) → fever bark (2080, contact) → opium tincture (2120) → ipecac (2172, contact) → foxglove (2370).
6. **Hospitals and licensing:** college of physicians (1932) → army field hospitals (1983) → nursing sisters (2066) → university clinical wards (2074) → general hospital (2112) → invalid soldiers' hospital (2140) → state medical college (2166) → subscription hospitals (2250) → surgeons' college (2288) → dispensaries (2348) → medical police code (2360) → fever houses (2376) → moral-treatment asylums (2382).
7. **Scurvy (shared: Nutrition, Logistics):** lemon juice (2006) → controlled trial (2296) → fleet citrus ration (2390).

**Ownership of overlaps.** Midwifery, forceps, caesarean birth, lying-in hospitals and foundlings are **Demography**'s. Corpse searchers (1990) stay here. Demography keeps the weekly bills they feed (2008). Keeping foods for voyages are **Nutrition**'s (2340). Cattle plague orders and the veterinary school are **Ecology**'s (`cattle_plague_slaughter_orders` 2226, `veterinary_schools` 2332). Cesspits, sewers, street pumps and water closets are **Infrastructure**'s. Microscopic animalcules are **Knowledge**'s (`animalcule_microscope` 2154). The seamen's hospital levy is **Labor**'s (2194). Institutions' police science (`police_ordinance_science` 2260) comes before the medical police code (2360), which writes health into it. Salaried town physicians are not repeated, because `town_physicians` (600–1200: 720) already covers them. `military_hospitals` (990) and `skin_flap_repair` (728) are continued, not repeated. **Security** supplies the guards for cordons and shut-up houses. `litter_bearer_drill` is placed here and shared with Security, because it gates the medical detachment in `military_unit_catalog.gd`. Microscopes and thermometers are **Knowledge**'s. This line keeps their medical use (2124, 2312).

## Government and civic life

These rows should visibly change government and civic life in the civic-evolution pass:
- **forty_day_quarantine (1822), health_passes (1832) and ship_bills_of_health (1921) [gov: law]:** quarantine becomes standing law. Every traveller and ship needs a health certificate, and gate and harbour officers check them.
- **quarantine_island_station (1845) [gov: towns]:** a walled quarantine station on an island or outside the harbour becomes a permanent civic building with a prior and guards.
- **standing_health_magistracy (1906) [gov: offices]:** the pestilence board of 1790 becomes a standing office that sits in plague-free years too and has its own officers, spies abroad and courts.
- **college_of_physicians (1932), state_medical_collegium (2166) and surgeons_college (2288) [gov: offices]:** medical colleges license and discipline practitioners. The state college sits in the capital under a minister.
- **printed_city_pharmacopoeia (1962) and chartered_apothecary_company (2036) [gov: towns]:** apothecaries must follow the printed town book and keep to their chartered company.
- **bathhouse_closures (1928), corpse_searchers (1990) and shut_up_plague_houses (2016) [gov: towns]:** the town shuts bathhouses, pays sworn searchers and puts watchmen at infected doors.
- **general_confinement_hospital (2112), subscription_hospitals (2250), outpatient_dispensaries (2348) and drowning_rescue_societies (2342) [gov: towns]:** hospitals governed by boards of subscribers and town governors become the largest charitable buildings in the town.
- **invalid_soldiers_hospital (2140) [gov: offices]:** a domed hospital and pension office for old soldiers is attached to the war ministry.
- **frontier_sanitary_cordon (2240) [gov: offices]:** a line of garrisoned quarantine posts along the frontier is run by a sanitary office under the army.
- **preventive_inoculation (2302) [gov: towns]:** towns pay inoculators, and the ruling house may be inoculated in public to set an example.
- **medical_police_code (2360) [gov: law]:** the state writes public health into law, covering food, water, burial, midwives and quarantine. District medical officers enforce it.
- **transfusion_ban (2136) [gov: law]** and **fleet_citrus_ration (2390) [gov: offices]:** single acts of state medical policy.
