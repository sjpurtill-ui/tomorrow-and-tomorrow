# Ecology: years 1800–2400

**Scope.** The game's **Ecology** research line (`ecology` dynamic; `Nature`-direction entries, the geoscience catalog, and the natural-history ids of the field-botany catalog) continues `y1200/ECOLOGY_1200_1800.md` and `registry_1800.json`. It covers woodland and fuel, land and water, herds and pasture, wildlife and fisheries, pollution, climate signals and natural history.

In this window, the land is reshaped by the great dying, by sheep and enclosure, by drainage and by contact across the ocean. Ecology becomes a state concern: realm forest ordinances, sustained yield, nuisance law, cattle-plague orders and forestry schools. Natural history turns systematic, from herbaria and comparative anatomy to classification, strata and deep time.

These belong to other lines:
- **Nutrition:** crop rotation (including the four-course rotation), the seed drill, marling and new-world crops.
- **Infrastructure:** sea walls, harbor works and town water.
- **Production:** coke and coal-fired furnaces.
- **Health:** quarantine, street cleaning and water filtration.
- **Knowledge:** the barometer, thermometer and microscope.

Rows marked `[gov: …]` change offices, law or towns. Names are generic practices. Real history is used only to calibrate dates.

**Historical anchor.** The `technology_eras.gd` CURVE on `origin/codex/research-600` runs `[[1500,1000],[2000,1600],[2400,1800]]` through this window. Game 1800 is about AD 1360, 1900 about AD 1480, 2000 about AD 1600, 2100 about AD 1650, 2200 about AD 1700, 2300 about AD 1750 and 2400 about AD 1800. In years 1800–2000, one game year is about 1.2 historical years. In years 2000–2400 it is half a historical year.
- **Years 1800–2000** cover the late-medieval crisis and its aftermath: woods and sheep over emptied fields, storm floods, cold margins, sheep enclosures, tillage laws, enclosure by agreement, and the first species exchange and island deforestation after contact.
- **Years 2000–2400** cover the early-modern analogs: water meadows, drained-peat shrinkage, imported timber, nuisance law, coal smoke, sustained-yield forestry, cattle plague, enclosure acts, classification, strata, the economy of nature and deep time.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Ecology team is working on the item. Real time is for **1 day/s**: 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** A plain `id` is in the main catalog today. Its year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through the CURVE, unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), `y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200), `y1200/registry_1800.json` (1200–1800), the game's baked blocks, `scripts/*.gd`, or another 1800–2400 list. "(continues: id)" names an earlier item that the row improves. "(shared: X)" marks an item that straddles into line X. Bands are ±40 years, and ±30 for key thresholds (bold).

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). It is `n/s` when the item is in main but was not reached in any recorded run, and `—` when it is not in main.

The total is **89** advances: 39 in years 1800–2100 and 50 in years 2100–2400. 16 of them are key thresholds. Across the seven channels, one lands about every 6–7 years. Most items are short customs, observations and ordinances (4–8 years). Long items (12–18 years) are state-scale drainage, realm ordinances and new sciences, and they are the key thresholds. The last century is the densest, because natural history, forestry and pollution law all mature in the historical span AD 1650–1800.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Woodland and fuel:** post-plague regrowth (1814) → yearly felling blocks (1838) → **arsenal oak reserves (1876)** → peat companies (1990) → timber imports (2100) → oak-planting treatise (2114) → **realm forest ordinance (2138)** → **sustained yield (2218)** → hedgerow timber (2242) → tree volume tables (2288) → firebreak rides (2316) → charcoal woods cleared (2356). The forestry academy is the 2400–3000 list's (`forestry_academy`, 2429). Coal and coke furnaces are Production.
2. **Land and water:** great drowning (1808) → dune grass laws (1948) → **floated water meadows (2050)** → covered field drains (2152) → peat shrinkage (2164) → dune pines (2312) → torrent check dams (2384). 3. **Enclosure and pasture:** sheep enclosures (1858) → **tillage protection laws (1910)** → **enclosure by agreement (1935)** → goat bans (1978) → pannage decline (2188) → cattle plague orders (2226) → cattle health passes (2234) → hedgerow timber (2242) → **assembly enclosure acts (2326)** → veterinary schools (2332). ## Government and civic life

These rows should change offices, law or towns at the seat of rule:
- **Salmon conservators** (1826), **arsenal oak reserves** (1876), the **realm forest ordinance** (2138) and **veterinary schools** (2332) `[gov: offices]` add forest masters, river bailiffs and animal doctors who answer to the ruler.
