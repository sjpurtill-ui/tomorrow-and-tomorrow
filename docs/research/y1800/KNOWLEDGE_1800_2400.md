# Knowledge: years 1800–2400

**Scope.** This file is the **Knowledge** line for game years 1800–2400 (`knowledge` dynamic; `direction: "Information"`). It has four channels: Observers, Directed attention, Preserved knowledge and Communication, and it continues `y1200/KNOWLEDGE_1200_1800.md`. It covers the press with cast metal type and its engraved plates, printed books, maps, news and primers, schools and universities, number from the new algebra to logarithms and the calculus, the sky from the sun-centred system to universal gravitation, the telescope, the microscope, the barometer and the air pump, the pendulum clock, the learned societies and their journals, the mechanics of motion and materials, early electricity, the new chemistry of airs, decimal measures and the shutter telegraph. Catalog ids placed here: `burin_engraving`, `copperplate_preparation`, `screw_press_printing`, `oil_based_printing_inks`, `hand_relief_printing`, `metal_type_casting`, `drypoint_printmaking`, `polynomial_equations`, `graphite_marking`, `complex_numbers`, `symbolic_algebra`, `rolling_intaglio_printing`, `measured_kinematics`, `lens_centering`, `logarithms`, `coordinate_geometry`, `mezzotint_printmaking`, `vacuum_pumps`, `probability_theory`, `vacuum_experiments`, `mechanical_oscillation`, `laboratory_notebooks`, `experimental_protocol_publication`, `numerical_root_finding`, `differential_calculus`, `experimental_optics`, `integral_calculus`, `elastic_deformation`, `eyepiece_design`, `stress_strain_relations`, `inertial_motion`, `momentum_balance`, `differential_equations`, `friction_measurement`, `precision_thermometry`, `structural_load_testing`, `flow_continuity`, `numerical_integration`, `column_buckling`, `experimental_controls`, `rotational_dynamics`, `calorimetry`, `flywheel_smoothing`, `feedback_governors`, `shutter_signal_frames`. The printing and intaglio ids carry the `Materials` direction in the catalog but were assigned to Knowledge by the 1200–1800 registry. Items owned by other lines are left out: the compass, sea charts, latitude sailing and the sea clock (Logistics); gunpowder, drill and fortification (Security); variolation, anatomy and hospitals (Health); herbals, botanic gardens and naming of living things (Ecology); paper mills, instrument trades and engines (Production); bills of mortality and life tables (Demography); offices, courts and press law (Institutions); literature, music, painting and theatre (Culture). Rows marked [gov: …] change government or civic life; the tag legend is in the Institutions file.

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` on `origin/codex/research-600` (`[[1500,1000],[2000,1600],[2400,1800]]`) maps game 1800 to ≈ AD 1360, 1900 to ≈ 1480, 2000 to AD 1600, 2100 to 1650, 2200 to 1700, 2300 to 1750 and 2400 to AD 1800. Years 1800–2000 run at 1.2 historical years per game year: the late-medieval crisis, the gunpowder transition, printing, the ocean voyages and the first contact exchanges. Years 2000–2400 run at 0.5 historical years per game year, so a historical decade takes 20 game years: the scientific revolution, the fiscal-military state and the age of reason, stopping at the threshold of steam power. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states, religions and events are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Knowledge team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3. The band after each year is ±35 years (±45 for bold key thresholds).

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE` unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), `docs/research/y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200), `docs/research/y1200/registry_1800.json` (1200–1800, aliases included) or a quoted id in `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names an earlier-registry item that a row improves, an earlier row of this file, or a row in another 1800–2400 line.

**"Today" column.** The earliest completion year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.
