# Health: years 2400–3000

**Scope.** The **Health** research line (`health` dynamic; `direction: "Health"`) for game years 2400–3000, the end of the game. It covers vaccines and immunisation, public health boards and notification, water treatment as a health measure, anaesthesia, antiseptic and aseptic surgery, germ theory, hospitals and nursing, the licensing of practitioners, drugs from alkaloids to antibiotics and engineered antibodies, imaging, field medicine for generals' armies, health insurance and national health services, and pandemic response. Birth, maternity and infant care belong to Demography and are marked shared where they touch Health. Diet, food safety and deficiency diseases belong to Nutrition. Pipes, sewers and waterworks belong to Infrastructure; `slow_sand_filtration` and chlorination are placed here as health measures and shared with Infrastructure. Microscopy and laboratory microbiology (`microbial_observation`, `cell_theory`, `instrument_sterilization`, `microbial_isolation_methods`, `aseptic_laboratory_practice`) are Knowledge's; germ theory and aseptic surgery here continue them. Continues `docs/research/y1200/HEALTH_1200_1800.md` and the 1800–2400 Health list. Catalog ids placed here: `slow_sand_filtration`, `water_service_inspections`, `contagion_mapping` and `nursing_care_organization` (all `belongs_later` or window items). `preventive_inoculation` is placed by the 1800–2400 Health list at 2302 (organised smallpox inoculation), and cowpox vaccination at 2394; compulsory infant vaccination here continues both. Also placed here, shared with Security: the field-medicine catalog set (`casualty_collection_posts`, `casualty_transfer_records`, `field_aid_stations`, `triage_registers`, `evacuation_relays`, `medical_resupply_packing`, `convalescent_duty_reviews`) and the treaty that protects the wounded. Field medicine changes what generals can sustain in the field; generals decide how to use it, and the player never commands units. Rows marked [gov: …] should be read together with the Institutions list in the civic-evolution pass.

**Historical anchor.** `scripts/technology_eras.gd` CURVE (read from `origin/codex/research-1200`) `[[2400,1800],[2800,1950],[3000,2030]]`: game 2400 ≈ AD 1800, 2500 ≈ 1838, 2600 ≈ 1875, 2700 ≈ 1912, 2800 = 1950, 2900 = 1990 and 3000 = 2030, the end of the game. Years 2400–2800 run at 0.375 historical years per game year (steam, railways, industrial chemistry, electricity and the world-scale industrial wars). Years 2800–3000 run at 0.4 (the antibiotic age to the near future). A game year is only four or five historical months, so bands are narrow: ±25 game years for ordinary rows and ±35 for key thresholds (about ±9 and ±13 historical years). Rows near the 2030 frontier are near-future practice; where they are not yet routine they are marked (speculative). None is science fiction. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real people, companies, nations, wars and events are calibration only. (regional) items suit a climate or tradition and should be gated by terrain or culture.

**Research time.** Game years of staffed research on the item. Real time is for **1 day/s** (1 game year ≈ 6 min; 5 y ≈ 30 min; 10 y ≈ 61 min; 20 y ≈ 2 h). At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today (`scripts/*.gd` or `technology_eras.gd` HISTORICAL_YEAR). Its year is its catalog year through CURVE unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW · slug` = not authored yet. No slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), `y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200), `y1200/registry_1800.json` (1200–1800), `scripts/*.gd` or HISTORICAL_YEAR; a script checks this. "(continues: id)" names the earlier item a row improves: an item from those registries, an earlier row of this file, or a row of a sister 2400–3000 line. `(shared: X)` straddles another line; `(culture)` feeds this line from Culture. `[gov: offices|court|law|seat|towns|culture]` marks rows that change government, civic life or the court.

**"Today" column.** Earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run; `—` for items not in main.

The total is **126** advances: 65 in years 2400–2700 and 61 in 2700–3000, one about every 4.8 game years (about every 2 historical years). 11 are catalog ids. Simple remedies, laws and instruments take 4–6 years (24–37 min). Institutions, drugs and techniques take 6–10 years. The 20 key thresholds take 10–20 years (1–2 h). The first century is vaccination at scale, drug alkaloids and clinical signs. The second is the sanitary idea: boards of health, anaesthesia, mapping cases to their source and organised nursing. The third is germ theory, antiseptic and aseptic surgery and antitoxins. After 2700 come world-war medicine, insulin, sulfa drugs and antibiotics, then national health services, controlled trials, vaccines against the childhood killers, oral rehydration and smallpox eradication. The line ends with antiviral therapy, the read human genome, pandemic vaccines designed in days and speculative broad vaccines held ready.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Vaccines:** 1800–2400's `preventive_inoculation` (2302) and `cowpox_vaccination` (2394) → vaccine carrier voyage (2408) → public vaccine stations (2416) → compulsory infant vaccination (2541) → laboratory-weakened vaccine (2627) → diphtheria antitoxin (2643) → paralysis-virus vaccine (2812) → one immunisation program (2860) → smallpox eradication (2872) → message-molecule vaccine (2975) → broad virus-family vaccines (3000, speculative).
2. **Public health and water:** 1800–2400's `town_fever_houses` (2376) → `slow_sand_filtration` (2411) → sanitary town survey (2512) → central board of health (2528) → `water_service_inspections` (2533) → `contagion_mapping` (2544) → medical officer of health (2547) → notification law (2637) → port inspection replaces blanket quarantine (2648) → water chlorination (2688) → inter-realm health council (2795) and rules (2938) → test, trace and lockdown (2975).

## Government and civic life

These should visibly change government and civic life in the civic-evolution pass:
- **public_vaccine_stations (2416)** and **compulsory_infant_vaccination (2541)**: a vaccinator becomes a public officer. Infant inoculation becomes a legal duty recorded by the registrar, and refusal becomes a civic dispute.
- **central_board_of_health (2528)**, **medical_officer_of_health (2547)** and **disease_notification_law (2637)**: a standing health ministry replaces the temporary pestilence magistracy. Every district gains a health officer with nuisance powers, and physicians must report cases.
- **medical_practitioner_register (2555)**, **national_pharmacopoeia (2459)**, **drug_label_purity_law (2696)** and **drug_efficacy_approval (2830)**: a medical council keeps the register, and a drug regulator licenses medicines before sale.
- **antipsychotic_drugs (2805)** and **community_mental_health (2832)**: the county asylum of the 1800–2400 list, by then a major civic building, gives way to community clinics.
- **worker_sickness_insurance (2621)** and **national_health_service (2795)**: sickness funds, then one national health service, become the largest civil office after defence. The court gains a minister of health.
- **neutral_wounded_convention (2571)**, **inter_realm_health_council (2795)**, **inter_realm_health_rules (2938)** and **tobacco_control_treaty (2932)**: treaties bind realms. Envoys to a standing health council appear in foreign affairs.
- **school_medical_inspection (2685)**, **pandemic_closure_orders (2715)**, **primary_health_care (2870)** and **pandemic_test_trace_orders (2975)**: town governments run school doctors, village health workers and emergency closure orders. In a pandemic year the health office overrides ordinary civic life for a time.
