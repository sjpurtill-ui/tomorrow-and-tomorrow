# Culture: years 2400–3000

**Scope.** This file is the **Culture** line for game years 2400–3000 (`culture` dynamic and direction). It has four channels: Social cohesion, Shared legitimacy, Inquiry breadth and Collective memory, and it continues `y1200/CULTURE_1200_1800.md` and the 1800–2400 Culture list. It covers national tongues and history lessons, great exhibitions and remembrance, the cheap press, the novel in its realist, serial and inner-voice forms, photography, recorded sound, film, radio and television as media of the public, sport and the revived games, reform movements (for temperance, for women's rights, for civil rights), mass leisure, youth music and the networked culture of the end of the game. The people revere their living god, so revivals, broadcast rites and critical study of the sacred texts are in the god's name. **No catalog id is placed here:** the only culture-dynamic id dated in this window, `public_libraries` (AD 1850), was already placed at 896; `endowed_town_libraries` continues it. Schools, laboratories and communication hardware are Knowledge. Offices, courts, law, franchise and state control of media are Institutions. Folk-song collections, the national flag and anthem, the public realm museum, the campaign against bond-slavery and the poetry of storm and plain speech are placed by the 1800–2400 Culture list and are not repeated. Rows marked [gov: …] change government or public opinion (see the tag legend in the Institutions file).

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` (`[[2400,1800],[2800,1950],[3000,2030]]`) maps game 2400 to AD 1800, 2500 to ≈ 1838, 2600 to ≈ 1875, 2700 to ≈ 1912, 2800 to 1950, 2900 to ≈ 1990 and 3000 to 2030, the end of the game. Years 2400–2800 run at 0.375 historical years per game year: steam, railways and the telegraph, industrial chemistry, electricity, mass politics and the first world-scale industrial wars. Years 2800–3000 run at 0.4 historical years per game year: computing, nuclear power, spaceflight, networks and the information age, ending in the near future of 2030. Items near 3000 are plausible extensions of what exists by the mid-2020s, not science fiction. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, companies, states, wars and religions are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Culture team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE` unless the last table gives a correction. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600), `docs/research/y600/registry_1200.json` (600–1200), `docs/research/y1200/registry_1800.json` (1200–1800, merge aliases included) or `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names an earlier item that a row improves, an earlier row of this file, or a row in another 2400–3000 line.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.

The total is **100** advances: 49 in years 2400–2700 and 51 in years 2700–3000. Across the four channels, one lands about every 6 years. Most items take 4–8 years; media thresholds (11 bold rows) take 8–12 years (0.8–1.2 real hours). The quiet stretch 2900–2950 (≈ AD 1990–2010) is the gap between the disc age and social networks.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Print and the public:** penny_daily_press (2488) → serialized_novels (2496) → political_cartoon_weeklies (2509) → million_reader_dailies (2667) → mass_paperbacks (2760) → photo_news_magazines (2763).

3. **Networked culture:** online_forums (2912) → file_sharing_networks (2922) → online_social_networks (2935) → algorithmic_feeds (2955) → networked_disinformation (2965) → generated_media (2982) → media_provenance_marks (2995) → shared_virtual_venues (3000).

5. **Movements:** temperance_societies (2469) → womens_rights_convention (2528) → common_ownership_doctrine (2528) → workers_festival_day (2637) → nonviolent_rights_movements (2832) → youth_counterculture (2845) → womens_liberation_movement (2850) → networked_protest_movements (2952).
6. **Art and letters:** historical_novel (2437) → realist_novel (2520) → open_air_light_painting (2597) → abstract_painting (2680) → inner_voice_novel (2725) → mass_image_art (2825).
7. **Play and leisure:** excursion_tourism (2547) → codified_ball_games (2568) → professional_sport_leagues (2627) → revived_realm_games (2656) → world_ball_cup (2747) → charter_flight_holidays (2825) → electronic_games (2855).
8. **The god in the modern age:** god_revival_meetings (2483) → sacred_text_criticism (2533) → broadcast_rites (2800) → remote_video_gatherings (2975). Reverence meets mass media rather than disappearing.
9. **Ownership of overlaps:** the hardware of photography, telephone, radio and networks is Knowledge; Culture owns their social use. State control of the press and broadcast is Institutions (`state_propaganda_ministry`); the chartered public broadcaster is here as a cultural body. Paid holidays and workers' leisure time are Labor. National parks and nature protection are Ecology; `world_heritage_list` covers cultural sites only. Air travel is Logistics; `charter_flight_holidays` is the leisure practice. The unconscious mind and the talking cure are Health (`talking_cure`, marked culture). Women's votes are Institutions (`womens_suffrage`); the movements are here.
