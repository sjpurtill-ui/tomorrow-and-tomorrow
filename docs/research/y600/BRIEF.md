# Years 600–1200 research lists — brief

Same format as the approved `docs/research/*_600_YEARS.md` (read KNOWLEDGE_600_YEARS.md first and match it): Scope, Historical anchor, Research time, Id column, Today column; two tables — Years 600–900 and Years 900–1200 — columns Year (band) | Discovery | Research | Real time | Id | Today; compact pacing summary; short "currently far too early / too late" table.

- Timeline (scripts/technology_eras.gd CURVE on origin/codex/research-600): game 600 ≈ 1500 BC, 800 ≈ 500 BC, 1200 ≈ AD 360 (interpolate 800→1500 as 500 BC→AD 1000). Late Bronze Age → early iron (iron smelting ~game 660+) → classical/Iron Age states → late antiquity analogs.
- ALTERNATIVE HISTORY: discovery names are generic, era-appropriate practices (no real places, people, empires, religions, or events in names; real history only as calibration).
- Continue from the 0–600 registry (`docs/research/registry.json`): never duplicate an existing registry item; you may list "improvements" of earlier items as new discoveries with distinct names; reference predecessors by registry id in a trailing "(continues: id)" note where helpful.
- Existing catalog ids beyond year 600 (scripts/*_knowledge.gd and technology_eras.gd HISTORICAL_YEAR, e.g. bloomery_smelting, iron_assaying, forge_welding, galley_navigation, caravanserais, public_theatre, craft_guilds, crossbow_mechanism…) must be placed in the window if they belong there (use their catalog id), or listed as "belongs later".
- Research time at 1 day/s (1 game year ≈ 6 real min; 6 y = 37 min). LONG lists (~70–100 per line), one short line each.
- Player is a god-figure; no victory framing; era-grounded.
