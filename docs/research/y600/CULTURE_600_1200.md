# Culture: years 600–1200

**Scope.** This file is the **Culture** line for game years 600–1200 (`culture` dynamic and direction). It has four channels: Social cohesion, Shared legitimacy, Inquiry breadth and Collective memory, and it continues `CULTURE_600_YEARS.md`. It covers court ritual and burial, epic and lyric, festivals and games, theatre and spectacle, sculpture, painting and mosaic, philosophy as a way of life, histories and biographies, and the rites of the god's house. The people revere their living god, so oracles, dedications, hymns and canons are addressed to the god. Among main-catalog culture ids, `civic_games`, `public_theatre`, `comparative_chronicles` and `public_libraries` are placed here. Schools, grammars and book media are in Knowledge (see Knowledge). Offices, courts and voting are in Institutions (see Institutions).

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` on `origin/codex/research-600` maps game 600 to ≈ 1500 BC, 800 to ≈ 500 BC and 1500 to AD 1000. Years 600–800 run at 5 historical years per game year: Late Bronze Age palaces, their collapse, then early iron (iron smelting from about game 660). Years 800–1200 run at ≈ 2.14 historical years per game year: classical city-states and leagues (800–900), large Iron Age kingdoms and learned cities (900–1000), then great territorial states and the start of late antiquity (1000–1200). By this curve game 1200 is ≈ AD 360. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states and religions are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Culture team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE`. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600) or in `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names the 0–600 registry item that a row improves.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.

The total is **87** advances: 48 in years 600–900 and 39 in years 900–1200. Across the four channels, one lands about every 7 years, so each channel always has work of its age. Most items take 4–12 years. Long thresholds take 12–20 years (1.2–2 real hours).

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Story and memory:** sung narrative of the god's deeds (580) → laments for the fallen palaces (676) → bards' fixed meter (700) → written epic (765) → histories by inquiry (823) → annals from the founding (1000) → commissioned founding epic (1025) → paired biographies (1086) → canon of the god's sayings (1190).
2. **The god's house:** pilgrimage (560) → oracle (730) → votive inscriptions (740) → columned stone house (780) → initiation rites (786) → contemplative schools (1150) → congregational hymns (1175) → congregation halls (1195).
3. **Contests and spectacle:** chariot races (642) → shrine games with truce (745) → civic games (760) → choral contests (792) → theatre (800) → stone theatres (880) → race-circus (1050) → amphitheatre (1060).
4. **Image and likeness:** gold masks (605) → life-size statues (778) → proportion canon (834) → true likeness (890) → emotional realism (950) → panel portraits (1082) → painted books (1200).
