# Demography: years 600–1200

**Scope.** The **Demography** research line (`demography` dynamic): births and child survival, marriage and inheritance, household and membership rules, counting people, and migration and settlement of newcomers. Healing belongs to Health and land law to Institutions; items that straddle are marked shared. Continues `docs/research/DEMOGRAPHY_600_YEARS.md`; nothing below duplicates `docs/research/registry.json`, and "(continues: id)" names the registry predecessor.

**Historical anchor.** `technology_eras.gd` CURVE (on `origin/codex/research-600`): game 600 ≈ 1500 BC, 700 ≈ 1000 BC, 800 ≈ 500 BC, 900 ≈ 290 BC, 1000 ≈ 70 BC, 1100 ≈ AD 140, 1200 ≈ AD 360 (the brief rounds 1200 to ≈ AD 570; the curve value is used here). The window runs from colony founding and household tax rolls through property censuses, written wills and midwifery manuals to child funds, life tables, hereditary trades and frontier settlement of migrating peoples. Names are generic practices; real history is only calibration. Aggregate population counts stay the simulation's truth; these items adjust fertility, survival, migration and household formation.

**Research time.** Game years of staffed Demography research. Real time at **1 day/s** (1 game year ≈ 6 min; 6 y ≈ 37 min; 10 y ≈ 61 min). At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** `id` = in the main catalog today (placed at its `HISTORICAL_YEAR`). `NEW` = not authored yet. `(shared: X)` straddles another line; `(culture)` feeds this line from Culture.

**"Today" column.** Earliest year seen in `docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`; `not seen` for catalog items no recorded run reached; `—` for items not in main.

## Pacing

| Years | 600–700 | 700–800 | 800–900 | 900–1000 | 1000–1100 | 1100–1200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advances | 14 | 13 | 13 | 12 | 11 | 10 |

The total is **73** advances: 40 in 600–900 and 33 in 900–1200. One lands about every 8 years. The main catalog holds only one demography item in this window (trained_midwives), so nearly the whole line is new authoring.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Counting people:** levy rolls (604) → household tax registers (732) → property census (804) → periodic census (936) → head-and-land units (1160).
2. **Settlement:** overseas colonies (618) → forced resettlement (668) → town consolidation (772) → mixed foundations (905) → federate settlement (1192).
3. **Births:** trained midwives (876) → midwifery manuals (920) → marriage incentives (1033) → child funds (1050).
4. **Membership and inheritance:** two-parent descent (700) → written wills (828) → service membership (1016) → universal membership (1130).
