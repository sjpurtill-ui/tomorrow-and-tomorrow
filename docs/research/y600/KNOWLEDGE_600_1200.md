# Knowledge: years 600–1200

**Scope.** This file is the **Knowledge** line for game years 600–1200 (`knowledge` dynamic; `direction: "Information"`). It has four channels: Observers, Directed attention, Preserved knowledge and Communication, and it continues `KNOWLEDGE_600_YEARS.md`. It covers letters and scripts, writing media, schools, calendars and sky tables, geometry and number, mechanics and measurement, maps and survey, and signals. Main-catalog math, mechanics and record-media ids whose `HISTORICAL_YEAR` falls in the window are placed at their curve year, give or take a few years. Philosophy schools used as communities, and written histories, go to Culture. Law schools and official examinations go to Institutions. Medicine, roads and minting belong to other lines and are only referenced here.

**Historical anchor.** The `CURVE` in `scripts/technology_eras.gd` on `origin/codex/research-600` maps game 600 to ≈ 1500 BC, 800 to ≈ 500 BC and 1500 to AD 1000. Years 600–800 run at 5 historical years per game year: Late Bronze Age palaces, their collapse, then early iron (iron smelting from about game 660). Years 800–1200 run at ≈ 2.14 historical years per game year: classical city-states and leagues (800–900), large Iron Age kingdoms and learned cities (900–1000), then great territorial states and the start of late antiquity (1000–1200). By this curve game 1200 is ≈ AD 360. Names are generic alternative-history practices. Real places, people, states and religions are used only for calibration.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Knowledge team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s** (speed setting 4). In real time, 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 10 game years are about 1 hour. At 3 days/s, divide by 3. At 8 h/s, multiply by 3.

**Id column.** A plain id is in the main catalog today; its game year comes from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` through `CURVE`. `NEW` = not authored yet. No `NEW` slug repeats an id in `docs/research/registry.json` (0–600) or in `scripts/*.gd`. "(continues: id)" names the 0–600 registry item that a row improves.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). "not seen" means the catalog item never completed in a recorded run. It is `—` when the item is not in main.

The total is **93** advances: 45 in years 600–900 and 48 in years 900–1200. Across the four channels, one lands about every 6 years, so each channel always has work of its age. Most items take 4–12 years. Long thresholds take 12–20 years (1.2–2 real hours).

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Script:** consonant sign-set (560) → letter order (628) → borrowed letter-set (700) → full vowel letters (740) → reading marks (944) → shorthand (1000) → cursive hand (1120).
2. **Media and books:** wax boards (640) → ink on hide rolls (680) → parchment (940) → rag pulping (1075) → paper (1082) → codex (1090) → catalogues, commentaries, epitomes (912–1196).
3. **Schools and inquiry:** schools of inquiry (800) → letter schools (810) → logic (872) → scholars' house (900) → higher curriculum (936) → salaried teachers (1076) → translation bureaus (1180).
4. **Number and geometry:** straightedge and compass (780) → demonstration (815) → incommensurables (840) → ratio, similarity, counting (893–897) → axiomatic geometry (899) → exhaustion (928) → trigonometry (963) → negatives (1030) → abbreviated algebra (1152).
5. **Sky and time:** eclipse records (705) → sky diaries (770) → eclipse prediction (805) → 19-year cycle (833) → star catalogue (976) → geared calculator (992) → leap-year calendar (1012) → sky-tables compendium (1108).
