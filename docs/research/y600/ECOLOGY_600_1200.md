# Ecology: years 600–1200

**Scope.** The game's **Ecology** research line (`ecology` dynamic; `Nature`-direction entries and the geoscience and field-botany catalogs) continues from the 0–600 list (`ECOLOGY_600_YEARS.md`, `registry.json`). It covers woodland and fuel, land, soil and water, herds and pasture, wildlife and fisheries, and pollution. Iron smelting from game year ~660 brings new fuel and slag pressures. Growing towns bring waste, smoke and the first state forests. Names are generic practices. Real history is used only to calibrate dates.

**Historical anchor.** On the `technology_eras.gd` CURVE (`codex/research-600`), game year 600 is about 1500 BC, 800 is about 500 BC, and 1200 is about AD 360. Between 800 and 1500, 500 BC–AD 1000 is interpolated, so one game year is about 2.14 historical years. Years 600–800 cover the Late Bronze Age and early iron: terrace upkeep, nesting-season bans, pannage and smelter fuel limits. Years 800–1200 cover classical and late-antique analogs: reserved ship-timber forests, written farming manuals and plant habitat treatises, fume flues, planned wetland drainage and forest supervisors.

**Research time.** Time is given in game years while a staffed Ecology team is working on the item. Real minutes are for **1 day/s**: 1 game year is about 6 minutes, and 6 years are about 37 minutes.

**Id column.** A plain `id` is in the main catalog today; a year from `HISTORICAL_YEAR` is converted on the CURVE. `NEW` = not authored yet. "(continues: id)" names a 0–600 registry predecessor. "(shared: X)" marks an item that straddles into line X. No row duplicates a registry id.

**"Today" column.** The earliest year seen in recorded headless campaigns (`docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`). It is `n/s` when the item is in main but was not reached in any recorded run, and `—` when it is not in main.

The total is **70** advances: 32 in years 600–900 and 38 in years 900–1200. Across the channels, one lands about every 8–9 years. Most items are short customs (5–8 years). The long ones (10–14 years) are state-scale works and written treatises, and they are marked as key thresholds.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Woodland and fuel:** smelter fuel watch (670) → charcoal licences (760) → dry-season fire bans (800) → ship-timber crown forests (850) → steep forests kept (880) → tree nurseries (890) → timber export limits (905) → managed city firewood rings (1100) → forest supervisors (1150) → forest tallies (1180).
2. **Land and water:** terrace upkeep (610) → thin soils left in grass (630) → well drawdown (715) → saline drains (860) → harbor silting linked to clearing (900) → check dams (1010) → planned wetland drainage (1065) → cross-slope ploughing (1195).
3. **Herds and pasture:** goats off regrowth (690) → pannage (725) → night folding (750) → fodder legumes (810) → hay meadows (830) → pasture fees (935) → drove roads (950) → matched breeds (980) → reseeding (1105).
4. **Wildlife and fisheries:** netting ban in nesting season (675) → wardens (780) → extirpation noticed (1000) → restocking (1000) → net mesh limits (1060); fish farming itself is in Nutrition.
5. **Pollution and towns:** slag capping (785) → dung collectors (845) → sewage fields (865) → smoky trades zoned (920) → tall smelter flues (1015) → tailing ponds (1085).
