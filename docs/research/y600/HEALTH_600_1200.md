# Health: years 600–1200

**Scope.** The **Health** research line (`health` dynamic; `direction: "Wellbeing"`): treatment, surgery, remedies, sanitation, public water and the organisation of healers. Birth and infant care live in Demography and are marked shared where they touch Health. Continues `docs/research/HEALTH_600_YEARS.md`; nothing below duplicates `docs/research/registry.json`, and "(continues: id)" names the registry predecessor.

**Historical anchor.** `technology_eras.gd` CURVE (on `origin/codex/research-600`): game 600 ≈ 1500 BC, 700 ≈ 1000 BC, 800 ≈ 500 BC, 900 ≈ 290 BC, 1000 ≈ 70 BC, 1100 ≈ AD 140, 1200 ≈ AD 360 (the brief rounds 1200 to ≈ AD 570; the curve value is used here). The window runs from written remedy compendia through iron instruments, case-observing healing schools, dissection, army hospitals and the grand medical syntheses to the first charity hospitals. Names are generic practices; real history is only calibration. (regional) items suit a different medical tradition and should be gated by culture or terrain.

**Research time.** Game years of staffed Health research. Real time at **1 day/s** (1 game year ≈ 6 min; 6 y ≈ 37 min; 10 y ≈ 61 min). At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** `id` = in the main catalog today (placed at its `HISTORICAL_YEAR`). `NEW` = not authored yet. `(shared: X)` straddles another line; `(culture)` feeds this line from Culture.

**"Today" column.** Earliest year seen in `docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`; `not seen` for catalog items no recorded run reached; `—` for items not in main.

## Pacing

| Years | 600–700 | 700–800 | 800–900 | 900–1000 | 1000–1100 | 1100–1200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advances | 16 | 15 | 15 | 14 | 13 | 12 |

The total is **85** advances: 46 in 600–900 and 39 in 900–1200. One lands about every 7 years per channel. Steps lengthen from 3–6 years for simple remedies to 15–20 years for anatomy and the grand synthesis, so the count per century falls while each step matters more.

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Observation:** fever crisis days (690) → fluid-balance theory (782) → healing schools (805) → daily case rounds (847) → medical synthesis (1160).
2. **Surgery:** iron probes (666) → cataract couching (736) → dissection (893) → vessel ligature (955) → ligated amputation (1028) → surgical anesthesia (1106).
3. **Public health:** marsh drainage (660) → town physicians (720) → flushed latrines (756) → civic infirmaries (893) → military hospitals (990) → charity hospitals (1195).
4. **Drugs:** dosed prescriptions (604) → materia medica (928) → drug shops (998) → fixed formulas (1098) → pharmacy weights (1186).
