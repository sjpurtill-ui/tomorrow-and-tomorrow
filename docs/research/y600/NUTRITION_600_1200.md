# Nutrition: years 600–1200

**Scope.** The **Nutrition** research line (`nutrition` dynamic; `direction: "Sustenance"`), channels Daily supply, Diet quality, Land productivity and Stored reserve. It covers iron-age farm tools, crop rotation, bread, oil and wine, dairy and preserved foods, milling, public grain and the first farming handbooks. Tools belong to Production and water works to Infrastructure; land practices the catalog tags as nutrition stay here and are marked shared. Continues `docs/research/NUTRITION_600_YEARS.md`; nothing below duplicates `docs/research/registry.json`, and "(continues: id)" names the registry predecessor.

**Historical anchor.** `technology_eras.gd` CURVE (on `origin/codex/research-600`): game 600 ≈ 1500 BC, 700 ≈ 1000 BC, 800 ≈ 500 BC, 900 ≈ 290 BC, 1000 ≈ 70 BC, 1100 ≈ AD 140, 1200 ≈ AD 360 (the brief rounds 1200 to ≈ AD 570; the curve value is used here). Late Bronze Age, then iron (iron tools from ≈ 660), classical farming states, and late-antique estates. All names are generic practices; real history is only calibration. Items marked (regional) suit monsoon-river or east-continental climates and should be gated by terrain.

**Research time.** Game years of staffed Nutrition research. Real time at **1 day/s** (1 game year ≈ 6 min; 6 y ≈ 37 min; 10 y ≈ 61 min). At 3 days/s, divide by 3.

**Id column.** `id` = in the main catalog today (placed at its `HISTORICAL_YEAR`, or at the placement the 0–600 review gave it). `NEW` = not authored yet. `(shared: X)` straddles another line.

**"Today" column.** Earliest year seen in `docs/technology-review/pacing/*.json`; `not seen` for catalog items no recorded run reached; `—` for items not in main.

## Pacing

| Years | 600–700 | 700–800 | 800–900 | 900–1000 | 1000–1100 | 1100–1200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advances | 15 | 17 | 16 | 16 | 12 | 10 |

The total is **86** advances: 48 in 600–900 and 38 in 900–1200. One lands about every 3.5 years; with the four channels in parallel, Nutrition always has an age-appropriate item open. Steps are longer than in 0–600 (4–15 years), and the key thresholds cluster around iron (660–670), leavening and rotation (700), the rotary quern (800) and water milling (895, 1012).

**Key thresholds:**
1. **Tillage:** iron ard shares (670) → crop rotation (700) → seed drill (858) → coulter plough (1100).
2. **Grinding:** rotary quern (800) → lever hopper mill (806) → water mill (895) → animal hourglass mill (905) → overshot wheel (1012).
3. **Bread:** leavened bread (700) → street bakeries (838) → graded flour (990) → public ovens (1170).
4. **Public food:** grain dole (818) → husbandry handbooks (864) → price-steadying granary (1004) → campaign rations (1020).
