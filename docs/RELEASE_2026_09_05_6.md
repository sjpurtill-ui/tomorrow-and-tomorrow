## Delivered

City intelligence opens beside the live city. Siege and city battle views share
its existing World3D and city mesh resources instead of constructing a replacement
city and ground patch. Assaults use the actual arriving army approach where known.
Battle actors sample actual terrain heights; the duplicate strategic army marker
is hidden during the encounter and restored when leaving. City and army camera
presets, drag-to-pan, right-drag orbit and cursor-anchored zoom remain available.

Friendly and enemy cloth colors stay consistent across map and battle, with
plus/cross standards and YOUR/ENEMY labels in addition to color. Weapons, skin,
horses, armor, unit types, animated attacks and bounded representative counts
remain intact. Generals receive matching standards and allegiance labels.

Occupation is a compact city-side interface with Government, Residents, Movement
and Control tabs. Policies explain settings and the real 30-day change lock before
commitment. Resident measures distinguish current values from predictions.
Transfers require a route/cost review that is invalidated when choices change.
Reinforcement uses arrived troops or directs the player to field-army movement.
Destruction, killing, forced labor and return of control have consequence review.
Recovery preparations and resistance instructions explain resources and timing
before commitment. The existing backend remains authoritative for all actions.

## Limits

This reuses the city's current world representation; it does not add house-level
pathfinding, physical gate breaches or historical snapshots of old city geometry.
Camera presets change framing; no continuous cinematic camera tween is claimed.
Battle outcomes still use the existing aggregate simulation and animated visual
representatives. Older outpost, vegetation and unfinished Blender prototypes stay
held. Source integration and a running player's loaded version are recorded
separately; launch only through the canonical launcher.
