# Release 2026.09.05.14 — The Alderford War

Enter the canonical game through tools/launch_game.ps1, then Military → Play General Campaign. This is an authored early war with two rivals. Ask your general a question, give an objective, then commit it. With live Terra configured, natural dialogue produces validated proposals; AI-off mode supports explicit commands and identifies its limitations.

Generals handle routes, food stops, formation execution, battle and retreat. Both rivals and the wider world advance during commitments and battles. Questions pause deliberation without issuing orders. An objection, explicit refusal, impossible route and unsupported request are distinct. Hidden personality and command succession affect behavior. Rivals retain goals, reports and conditional plans; model calls occur at consequential events rather than every tick. Difficulty changes adaptation and planning, not resource rules.

The screen shows actual terrain, dated intelligence, clear force/food/equipment/casualty counts, conversation and reports. Recorded battles use the resolved forces and losses. Drag to pan, right-drag to orbit and wheel to zoom at the pointer. Core controls fit 800×600. Campaign saves and returning to the prior world are available.

## Validation

- 56/56 tests across general campaign, military development, city intelligence, city force capacity and scout return speed.
- Complete campaign replays: a defensive/recovery policy wins; reckless advance loses Alderford despite surviving soldiers. Actual shared simulation, finite supplies, casualty pools and required garrisons.
- 72 deterministic combat strategy replays, including held-out seeds. Held-out prepared defense averaged 25.5 own losses versus 83.7 for prepared assault and 91.2 for undersupplied assault. This validates those scenarios, not universal AI quality.
- Live Terra: six player exchanges and one opponent planning request, all seven HTTP 200 and accepted. Actual usage 9,309 prompt + 818 completion = 10,127 tokens; roughly 2.71 seconds mean latency. No estimated dollar cost. Questions did not execute missions; revised orders produced validated drafts.
- Final 15/15 general tests re-run after report changes; both complete campaign outcomes re-run. The defensive headless replay reports one resource still in use at process shutdown; it reaches its asserted terminal outcome.
- Actual GPU campaign controls, battle camera and small-window journey; actual entry/return journey; campaign save/reload and pre-feature save compatibility probes.

Evidence is in the feature worktree artifacts directory (general-combined-release.log, general-ui-final.log, general-entry.log, general-defensive-final.log, general-reckless-final.log, general-strategy-replays.log, general-live-final.log and general-old-save.log). Generated logs and isolated save overrides are not source files.

## Boundaries

This finite authored slice is not natural campaign unlocking or a complete all-era military system. Movement uses hidden 2 km commitments; there is no naval/global route solver. Opponent memory and political authority are bounded, not a full institutional model. Seven live requests verify the dialogue contract, not long-run personality or novice usability. Conversation history may scroll; primary controls remain visible. Existing continuous-world first-contact limitations remain in PLAYER_JOURNEY_BACKLOG.md.
