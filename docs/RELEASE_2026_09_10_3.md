# 2026.09.10.3 — Knowledge travels with people

Exploration brings home real samples, objects, knowledge and culture. Knowledge workers study them, and evidence can change the route and timing of research. Fifteen alternative early research routes reconverge on ordinary discoveries.

Recruitment invites existing households from known communities. They compare living conditions, need provisions, and reserve actual reception places. Arrivals take administrative work to settle and can strain housing, cohesion and health. Keeping people home leaves them available for ordinary work and research.

Leaders respond to those conditions, to practices learned from other societies and to people leaving. Cultural exchange affects cooperation. Research agreements support both participants; border understandings pause mutual recruitment. Player and opponent civilizations use the same rules.

Open **Scouting → Objects, knowledge & culture** to see the collection and set reception/sharing policy. Research inspectors explain alternative approaches and the source of learned practices.

See `KNOWLEDGE_EXCHANGE_HANDOFF.md` for validation, compatibility and limits. This release does not fix every mature-campaign frame stall or establish full 2,500-year balance. Existing saves begin recording the new dynamics from their next simulation steps.
